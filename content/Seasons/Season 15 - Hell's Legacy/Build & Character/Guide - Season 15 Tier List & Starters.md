> [!d4-s15] Tier Lists & Season Starters — Day 2
> Every list below is a **Sep 15 (day-1) artifact** — re-verify ≈Sep 30 · Rogue = safest start, Blazing Scream Warlock = highest ceiling · Maxroll's S tier is 7 builds across 7 different classes · the four lists do not agree on Warlock, Sorcerer, Spiritborn, Paladin or Druid

## What these rankings are worth right now

All four lists landed on **Sep 15, 2026 — launch day** — and none has been revised since. Maxroll's overall endgame list carries a "Last Updated: September 15, 2026" stamp and a Sep 15 changelog entry ("Big updates to Warlock positions per newest patch notes. — Macrobioboi"); Icy Veins self-describes as "an early snapshot"; Rhykker and Rob2628 both published on day 1. Treat everything here as `community-consensus (day 2)` and re-check around **2026-09-30**.

The dominant uncertainty is the **PTR→live gap**, and three independent sources say so explicitly: wudijo ("kind of how it goes"), Rob2628 ("Just take everything with a grain of salt, guys. No one has played this final patch note yet"), Rhykker ("I'm less confident than usual in the final rankings"). Any PTR-sourced DPS claim is downgraded on this page. Patch context: 3.2.1, build 73552, launched 2026-09-15 ~18:30 CEST — see [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Reference - Season 15 Class Changes 3.2.1|Season 15 Class Changes 3.2.1]] for the numeric changes behind these movements.

## Where the lists disagree

Presented as-is. These are not reconciled here — the structural reason for most of them is in the next section.

| Conflict | Position A | Position B | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warlock** | Maxroll: S-tier build + a dedicated Sep 15 Warlock re-rank; Rob2628: **#1 class and #1 build** | Rhykker: **mid-B tier** — strong Pit push, but "the warlock is just slower than any other class" on speed farming and leveling | Open. Build ceiling vs class breadth |
| **Sorcerer** | Maxroll: **Firewall Sorc in S** | Rhykker: **weakest class in the game**, bottom of B — "the Sorcerer no longer has competitive speed farming options" | Open. Same breadth penalty |
| **Spiritborn** | Maxroll S ×2; Rhykker **#1 overall class** | Rob2628: only "decent… not as strong as before" — he is the only one still weighing the pre-fix ~6× Sick Rune damage | Open. Rob is the outlier |
| **Paladin** | Maxroll A/B; Rob2628 "middle field, not the strongest"; Rhykker B | Icy Veins: **large S block** for speed farming, bossing and endgame | Open. **Icy Veins is the outlier** — Maxroll, Rhykker and Rob all read "buffed from a low base, improved but not top" |
| **Druid** | Maxroll: **Shred Druid in A**, Lacerate the headline build | Rob2628: "Druid, especially Shred, has been nerfed like crazy. Landslide is still pretty decent" | **Unresolved** — opposite readings of the same patch |
| **Barbarian** | — | — | *Not actually a disagreement:* Maxroll (S Whirlwind), Rhykker (A), Rob ("Whirlwind is still a true powerhouse… very safe to start") all agree he is **nerfed but alive**. Any "Barbarian is finished" framing contradicts all three |
| **Necromancer** | Maxroll S (Blood Wave) + Rhykker A | Rob2628: **last of eight** | Open. Rob is the outlier |

### Class-level vs build-level — the methodology gap

This is the single biggest source of the apparent contradictions, and the lists are **not mergeable into one ranking**:

- **Maxroll and Icy Veins rate builds.** A build can sit in S on raw ceiling even if it is the only playable option in its class. Maxroll's S tier spanning seven different classes is a build-level outcome, not a claim that seven classes are equally strong.
- **Rhykker rates classes**, and explicitly penalises classes that have one build and no alternatives. That is why **Firewall Sorc (S as a build) and Sorcerer (weakest class) are both correct**, and why **Warlock lands mid-B despite "arguably the best pit pushing class"** — no speed-farm or leveling game. He also abandoned F tier this season: "there's no bad class. No class fails… probably one of the best states of class balance we've had in a while." *(confidence: community-consensus)*
- **Rob2628 rates builds and classes together** but weights single-target DPS unusually heavily because the Prime Evils return as bosses — "it seems like single target DPS this season is very important with these new bosses." That is his stated reason for putting Warlock first. *(speculation — his own expectation, encounter frequency unknown)*

Practical read: if you are picking **one build for one job**, use Maxroll/Icy Veins. If you are picking **one class to play all season and want options**, Rhykker's structure is the more honest one.

**Leveling picks diverge by source.** Icy Veins' S leveling list: Whirlwind + HotA Barbarian, Blessed Hammer Paladin, Dance of Knives Rogue, Quill Volley Evade + Payback Spiritborn, Charged Bolts Sorcerer, Demon Summon + Abyss Rampage Warlock, Summoner Necromancer. Rhykker's: Blizzard Sorcerer, Frenzy Throw Barbarian, Dance of Knives Rogue, Minion Necromancer, Apocalypse Warlock. *(both `speculation` — single-source lists)* **Only Dance of Knives Rogue appears on both.**

**Leveling meta shift that changes starter value:** Undercity is now the recommended 1–70 path — unlock Warplans at 25, then chain Undercity runs for XP globes — **after Helltide and the worms were nerfed** (wudijo, Sep 16). Leveling builds that cannot clear Undercity bosses cleanly lose value versus last season's Helltide leveling; see [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Guide - Season 15 Leveling 1-70|Season 15 Leveling 1-70]] and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]].

**Splinter choice is a build decision, not a flat buff.** Take **Baal/Destruction first** (boosts Undercity, includes a 50% XP bonus — the current best leveling strategy), then **Mephisto/Hatred** (Pit + Nightmare Dungeons, run treated "as if you were 10 tiers higher", glyph leveling to 150 feasible on weaker builds). **Diablo/Terror is weak** by comparison — it feeds Infernal Hordes and Helltides, both just nerfed. Choice is swappable later, not permanent (Torment-6 gate reported, unconfirmed). Full detail in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]] and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes|Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes]] *(wudijo, Cliptis, Sep 16)*.

## Coverage gaps — where the guide supply is thin

- **Mobalytics is not a usable decision source this week.** Its own page: "Due to our team still travelling from BlizzCon, the Tierlist will be further updated shortly after Season 15 launches." Page metadata **still reads Season 13 / Patch 3.0.1** while the header says Season 15 — stale and internally mismatched. Its runeword guidance is also deferred: "our guides will be updated with them as soon as they have been fully discovered."
- **Sorcerer** — effectively one supported build (Firewall). Everything else is B–D on Maxroll (Meteor, Ball Lightning, Ice Shards, Hydra, Fireball, Frozen Orb, Incinerate — with Frozen Orb and Incinerate in D). Highest guide-thinness relative to class strength; and per Rhykker no competitive speed-farming option exists at all.
- **Druid** — **no S-tier entry anywhere in this cluster.** Maxroll's highest Druid builds are A; Rhykker puts the class in B. Rhykker also notes **no overlap between Druid speed builds (Cataclysm, Lacerate) and pushing builds (Landslide, Storm Strike)**, i.e. there is no must-have Druid build.
- **Warlock leveling lags behind the class's power.** Despite being the consensus strongest class, Maxroll published only the **Blazing Scream Warlock Leveling Guide** (Sep 16), and Rob2628 said his leveling planner was still in progress ("I'm still working on leveling planner… the Dread Claws one is already ready"). *(Rob's auto-transcript artifact — see below.)*
- **Paladin** — paradoxically the **best-documented** class on Maxroll (largest guide count, mostly aoinomiku-authored) and the **least settled in strength**. Guide supply exceeds consensus on power.
- **Creator skew:** no creator in this cluster covers **Druid or Sorcerer** at depth. P4wnyhof is Warlock-only (and German-language, stream-format); aoinomiku is a single-class Barbarian specialist who is also a Maxroll author (Zeal/Divine Lance/Zenith Paladin, Lunging Strike and Whirlwind Barbarian).
- **Barbarian splinter/unique interactions are the weakest-covered area of the whole cluster** — aoinomiku's "Is Whirlwind still the top dog?" has transcripts disabled, so his S15 Barbarian data is unread.

## Unresolved — do not treat as settled

- **Black Soul Splinter × Warlock demon-consumption.** Whether the splinter still counts when consuming demons is undecided: "we don't know exactly what's going to happen" — and if it does, "warlock is by far by far the best class" (Rob2628). **Rob's Warlock #1 verdict is explicitly conditional on this.** This is the single biggest swing factor in the whole ranking and should be re-tested after the first week. *(speculation)*
- **Rogue/Warlock core-stat scalar.** Only five classes are enumerated in the patch notes as scalar-changed (Barbarian 1.1 → 0.8; Druid, Necro, Paladin, Sorcerer all 1.25 → 1.625). **Rogue and Warlock are not listed** — read as unchanged, `community-consensus`, not `confirmed`. If Warlock's was raised, the Warlock-#1 consensus strengthens; if Rogue's was, Rogue's position does.
- **Rob2628's Sorcerer slot is an ASR artifact.** His spoken order "Druid, Swag, Necro" is transcribed as "Druid, Sorc, Necro", so the Sorcerer placement in his ranking should be re-checked against his published d4builds.gg cheat sheet before hard-coding it.
- **Rhykker's Barbarian A-tier placement is inferred, not quoted.** The transcript says "we got two classes here [A tier] and we're going to start with the necromancer", then "Next in the B tier, we have the barbarian" — but B tier was closed out with "the last B-tier class is the paladin" and A needs two classes. Read as a caption/ASR slip. `speculation` on the Barbarian placement only.
- **Splinter of Hellfire × Ring of Red Furor** (+100% damage / −100% Crit Chance paired with the buffed Red Furor, 100 → 30 Fury requirement) with a claimed Pit 140 ceiling is **low-trust and PTR-based** — uncorroborated by Maxroll, wudijo or Rob. Lead only. *(speculation)*
- **Barbarian's net damage loss has no reliable number.** Sources given range from ~20% at 3,000 Strength to "around half" to "none of it knocks the class out of contention" — no post-launch measurement exists. No figure is quoted here deliberately.
