> [!d4-s15] Season 15 Leveling 1–70
> 3.2.1 nerfed the Helltide maggot opening while **doubling** Undercity Jade Epiphany orbs · day-2 default: War-Plan Helltide to 24, Undercity Jade Epiphany to 70 · capstones ~35 and 50–60 · Rogue safest starter, Blazing Scream Warlock highest ceiling *(community-consensus, day 2 — no post-nerf guide has re-rated the route yet)*

## Route at a glance

| Level band | Route | Key actions | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–14 | War-Plan-driven Helltide | Rebirth or new seasonal → Temis → start a War Plan, reroll toward Helltide-heavy → Mercenary + Pet + Horadric Cube + seasonal quest | community-consensus |
| 1–70 (any) | Whispers layer | Turn in at **10 Grim Favors**; both Fields of Hatred world bosses (Alzuuda / Denshar) give **5 each** while their Whisper is up = one turn-in | community-consensus |
| 15–24 | Helltide, in-plan | Complete the **displayed** objective — opening any chest is not always enough | community-consensus |
| 25+ | **Default: Kurast Undercity — Jade Epiphany node** | Beacon/Attunement to **Attunement Rank 4** → collect orbs yourself → boss → repeat | community-consensus |
| 25+ (alt) | Helltide maggot/Hellwyrm farm | Only if the nodes are already levelled and maggots spawn reliably | community-consensus |
| ~35 | First Capstone Dungeon | Timing window ~30–40, **~35 typical** | community-consensus |
| 50–60 | Second Capstone | Gated with Season Rank objectives | community-consensus |
| 70 | Paragon activates | Then Pit Tier 10 → Torment 1 → T1–5 gear/Aspects → T6–9 Paragon/Glyphs → T10–12 Masterwork | community-consensus |

Difficulty at the start: **Normal**, moving to **Hard** when packs die quickly (D4Guides, Sep 9). Maxroll's S14 speed guide says start on Hard — the two disagree, and the D4Guides line is the S15-dated one.

## What 3.2.1 changed in the opening

The patch, not the guides, is authoritative here — every leveling guide in circulation was written pre-launch (D4Guides Sep 9, Maxroll Jun 30, Switchblade May 4) and still describes the maggot farm as strong.

| Change | Direction | Effect on the route |
|---|---|---|
| "Reduced the chance for **Plague Maggot Young** spawned by the **Hellmouth** Helltide node to drop Experience Orbs" | nerf | The S14 day-1 maggot-spam opening is materially worse |
| "Portent of Pain and Plague Devourers now drop **1–2** Experience Orbs instead of **always** dropping 2" | nerf | Helltide orb yield down on every non-maggot elite too |
| "**Doubled** the number of Experience Orbs spawned by the **Jade Epiphany** Undercity node" | buff | Undercity becomes the faster XP node — the compensating buff |
| War Plans baseline Experience Orbs per completed node **2 → 4** at Torment X+, **regardless of reward type or rarity**; Magic/Rare/Legendary/Mythic Experience-node orbs doubled again | buff | Post-70, not the 1–70 legs |
| **Expert and Penitent unlock after the Prologue** instead of requiring campaign completion | unlock | Earlier access to the higher-XP difficulties for the chest turn-in trick |
| The Pit: Progress-Orb XP **doubled** with **Choron's Soul** active; higher-tier bonus XP up (**T150: 1800% → 2700%**) | buff | Post-70; also undercuts any flat "Pit = B-tier" ranking from S13 |
| Whispers: cache orbs **3–5 → 4–6** plus **+2–4 at Torment X+** | buff | Passive layer, scales late |

Source for the delta block: 3.2.1 patch notes as reproduced by Vitablo (Sep 12) and Sportskeeda (Sep 14), summarised by MP1st (Sep 15) — `confirmed`, they agree with each other. **Caveat:** Blizzard's own patch-notes page (news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24287406) still only rendered up to 3.1.3 (Aug 12) during the sweep, so no 3.2.1 number came from Blizzard's page directly.

## Route A — War-Plan Helltide, 1–24

- **Start:** Rebirth or a new seasonal character — both begin at **Level 1**. Rebirth keeps class/name/appearance and is the S15 headline shortcut; move gear to the Eternal stash first, it does not carry. (D4Guides, Sep 9; Maxroll season guide, Sep 13)
- Go straight to **Temis**, start a **War Plan** with a Helltide-heavy chain (use the rerolls), then pick up Mercenary + Pet, check the Horadric Cube, and start the seasonal quest. (D4Guides, Sep 9)
- Run the plan's Helltide objectives and complete the **displayed** objective — opening any chest is not always enough. (D4Guides, Sep 9)
- **Hellmouth → Writhe and Rot(e)** are the named Helltide nodes to target; the XP-node path is Helltide's Centre Path (4 pts) → Right Path (3 pts) per the S13-era Switchblade article — `speculation` for S15, no node redesign shipped in 3.2.1. (Switchblade Gaming, May 4, S13-verified)
- **Whisper layer runs concurrently on every route** — 10 Grim Favors per turn-in; Alzuuda and Denshar world bosses contribute 5 each while their Whisper is up, not repeatable until the Whisper reactivates, PvP risk applies. (D4Guides, Sep 9)

**Why the old maggot farm is no longer the default:** Maxroll's S14 TLDR and the vault's own S13 War Plans note both treat Helltide maggot farming as a top opening. 3.2.1 nerfed exactly those orbs (see table above). Trust the patch notes over any pre-launch guide on this point.

## Route B — Kurast Undercity Jade Epiphany, 25 → 70

- **At Level 25 you choose the route.** Undercity (Jade Epiphany) is the default pick for pure speed to 70; stay on the developed Helltide maggot/Hellwyrm farm **only** if its nodes are already levelled and maggots spawn reliably. (D4Guides, Sep 9; Maxroll S14 TLDR, Jun 30)
- **Loop:** Beacon/Attunement to **Attunement Rank 4** → collect the experience orbs **yourself** (do not rely on the Pet) → boss → repeat. Rushing the boss misses the bonus. (D4Guides, Sep 9; Maxroll S14 guide confirms the same Jade Epiphany rush and that node XP scales with difficulty)
- **Pre-Unlock**, just complete activities fast to earn the first point — roughly **4 activity completions**.
- **Helltide fallback loop (Route B′):** build Threat → Hellwyrm spawns → maggots spawn → collect orbs manually → chests → next spawn. Do not over-push difficulty early; Helltide elites kill undergeared characters. (D4Guides, Sep 9)
- **Undercity node priority** carried from S13: **Gutter Filth First** (`speculation` for S15 — no post-3.2.1 node-value re-review exists in this sweep).
- **Build consequence of the route flip:** leveling builds that cannot clear Undercity bosses cleanly lose value versus last season's Helltide leveling. (wudijo, Sep 16, day 2 — `community-consensus`)

**Splinter choice interacts with the route.** wudijo's recommendation is **Baal/Destruction first** (it amplifies Undercity, the current best leveling strategy, and grants a **50% XP bonus** among its seasonal bonuses), then **Mephisto/Hatred** for the Pit and Nightmare Dungeons; **Diablo/Terror is the weak pick** because it feeds Infernal Hordes and Helltides, both just nerfed. Each Prime Evil splinter also gates which War Plan reputation nodes are reachable, so the pick changes which seasonal bonuses you can take. (wudijo, Sep 16 — `community-consensus`, day 2). Full detail in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]] and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes|Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes]].

**Splinter of Lies:** gives bonus XP but keeps you **permanently Weakened and Vulnerable**, equippable only when you own it — not part of a guaranteed early route. (D4Guides, Sep 9, explicit PTR caveat — `speculation`)

## Capstones, difficulty gates and the Pit unlock

| Milestone | Timing | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Expert / Penitent world tiers | After **Prologue completion** | `confirmed` (3.2.1) — previously required campaign completion |
| First Capstone Dungeon | ~Level **30–40**, **~35 typical** | `community-consensus` |
| Second Capstone Dungeon | ~Level **50–60**, gated with Season Rank objectives | `community-consensus` |
| Pit unlock gate | In S14 it came from **Season Rank 2** | `speculation` — D4Guides states this is **unconfirmed for S15**; verify in-game |
| Level 70 | Paragon activates | `community-consensus` |

Post-70 ladder (D4Guides, Sep 9): **L70 / early Paragon** = fill gear gaps, Temper, first important Paragon node → **Pit Tier 10 + Torment 1** → **T1–5** = core Aspects, required Uniques, Runes, Glyphs → **T6–9** = Paragon, Glyphs, Talisman bonuses, defenses → **T10–12** = Masterwork and optimize. World tier bonus XP moved in 3.2.1: **T10 1200% → 1300%, T11 1300% → 1500%, T12 1400% → 1700%**.

The **endgame capstone that unlocks Torment** is the hardest Day-1 gate (Torment = Greater Lair Boss runs, Mythic drops, Resplendent Sparks). A Boosting/RMT site (`low-trust`) frames this as "every Challenge Dungeon portal / Greater Lair Boss on Day 1" — that specific sequencing is **not** corroborated; the Torment unlock and Splinter socketing themselves are. See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]] and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Guide - Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy|Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy]].

## What is not settled

- **Maxroll has published NO S15 leveling route.** Its Speed Leveling Guide is last updated **June 30, 2026** and its TLDR still reads **"Fastest Leveling in Season 14"** (`confirmed` as a statement about the guide's date). Treat every Maxroll leveling specific as S14-carried-over until they re-date it — including the S14 route: Helltides + Nightmare Dungeons, fish **two Helltide War Plans at level 1**, farm Cinders on a safe difficulty then swap to **Penitent** for the chest turn-in, **gamble 2/3 of Level-25 plans into Undercity**.
- **The 1–70 route above leans on D4Guides' PTR-based, self-declared provisional guide** (Sep 9) plus Maxroll's S14 route. The S14→S15 delta (maggot nerf, Jade Epiphany buff) is `confirmed` by patch notes, but **no guide has re-rated the route after it**.
- **Helltide/worm Paragon-farming nerf is asserted, not measured** — wudijo: "I'm not 100 times sure where exactly the balance is right now because I haven't done the Helltide, but it was seemingly quite heavily nerfed from the patch notes." Not settled.
- **Runewords were shadow-dropped in S15** and a **Stealth** runeword (Tal + F, +movement speed) crafts in the Horadric Cube from a stripped normal item and is recommended for early leveling — `community-consensus (day 2)`; nobody has fully mapped the system yet.
- **Splinter swap timing:** reported locked for a while after the quest with a Torment-6 gate to change it — reported, not self-verified by the reporter. See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Reference - Season 15 New Systems|Season 15 New Systems]].
- **Re-verify ~2026-09-30** once wudijo and Maxroll publish post-launch leveling and War Plans content.
- Nightmare Dungeon affix selection during the pre-35 legs matters less than last season (the route is Undercity-shaped), but the reference stays current if you detour: [[Mechanics/Nightmare Dungeon Affixes|Nightmare Dungeon Affixes]].
