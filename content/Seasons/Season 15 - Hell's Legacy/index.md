## Quick Reference: What's New in Season 15

| Change | Notes |
|---|---|
| Season theme | 30th anniversary season. Prime Evils return — including Diablo (the Lord of Terror) — plus Deckard Cain, anniversary rewards, and gear inspired by every era of the franchise |
| Splinters of Evil | NOT the same thing as Soul Splinters — Blizzard named two unrelated systems almost identically. Three Prime Evil picks (Diablo, Baal, Mephisto) driving the reputation track and amplifying one activity family each: Diablo→Helltide/Infernal Hordes, Baal→Undercity/Lair bosses, Mephisto→Pit/Nightmare Dungeons. Swappable; the largest Waking Nightmares drop the rare resource that allows a swap |
| Soul Splinters | Eight socketable jewelry gems, max 3 equipped, each granting stats/resistances plus a signature effect with a drawback. Same-type/same-rarity splinters amalgamate in the Horadric Cube to the next rarity. Rarity tier count conflicts between Blizzard's PTR blog (5, incl. Common) and launch blog (4) — unresolved |
| Waking Nightmares | Three types: multi-level capstone, fixed-location standard dungeon, and sudden one-room overworld portals (~1 min life; Helltide the common spawn). Launch difficulty selection is via in-room braziers — the PTR's "three Torment tiers above your rank" line was not repeated at launch |
| Rebirth | Convert an existing Eternal-realm character into a Seasonal one — keeps name, appearance, and cosmetic loadout, resets to level 1. Character must be stripped of carried items first |
| Legacy Uniques | Nine new uniques drawn from Diablo, Diablo II, and Diablo III |
| Unique Charms | 16 new unique Charms based on previously unrepresented uniques, including variants for eight legacy uniques. Charms can now roll at mythic rarity (max-rolled stats, buffed modifier). No crafting method and no target farming |
| Mythic crafting | Horadric Cube "upgrade to mythic" is now deterministic — converts the input item directly into its matching mythic with affixes and crafted modifications. Random factors removed. Not usable on unmodifiable items |
| Iconic Mythics | The PTR plan for Unique-quality Iconic Mythics was **reverted at launch** — Iconic Mythics stay Mythic-quality only. A second Blacksmith Iconic-only cache was added to compensate for the streamlined route the PTR had removed |
| Runewords | Shadow-dropped at launch and absent from the 3.2.1 notes — D2-style runewords (Enigma, Grief, Infinity, Spirit, and more) craft by combining a normal base item with runes in the Horadric Cube. Day-2 and datamined; the recipe list traces to one source (Icy Veins), with Maxroll's season guide mirroring it rather than confirming it independently — treat the mapping as speculation, the system itself as community-consensus |
| Horadric Post | One-way delivery channel that sends rewards, supplies, and commemorative items to your account. No player-to-player sending |
| Platforms | Diablo IV launched on Nintendo Switch 2 the same day as the season (Age of Hatred Collection) |
| Solo Self-Found | Unavailable at launch — login-blocking issues were hotfixed on September 15 and the Seasonal Realm reopened; SSF remains disabled pending investigation |

*Sources: Blizzard News (3.2.1 patch notes, BlizzCon 2026 recaps), Icy Veins launch-day hotfix coverage. Figures from the 3.2.0 PTR are flagged where they may have changed at launch.*
