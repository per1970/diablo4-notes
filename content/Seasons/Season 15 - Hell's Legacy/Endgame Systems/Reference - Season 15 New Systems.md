> [!d4-s15] Season 15 New Systems
> Rebirth turns an Eternal character into a level-1 Seasonal one — strip it first, cosmetics carry only as saved ensembles · nine legacy Uniques are permanent on Eternal *and* Seasonal · 16 new Unique Charms + 7 legacy variants, any of which can drop as a max-rolled Mythic Charm with **no crafting or target farming** · Runeword Crafting shadow-dropped at launch and appears in **no** Blizzard source.

## Rebirth — Eternal → Seasonal conversion

Converts an **existing Eternal-realm character** into a Seasonal one: same character, reset to **level 1**, no new character screen. (Blizzard season blog, Sep 15; previewed in the 3.2.0 PTR blog, Jul 30)

| Carries over | Lost or blocked |
|---|---|
| Class, name, appearance, Normal/**Hardcore** state | All gear and items |
| Cosmetic preferences — transmogs and pigments, mounts and accessories, pets, portals, headstones — **only when saved as part of an ensemble** | Personal inventory contents; currencies are also removed (Icy Veins) |
| A running tally of hours played across *all* Rebirths of that character | Current level (reset to 1) |

- **Pre-flight requirement:** Rebirth is **blocked until the character has nothing equipped and nothing in its personal inventory**. The warning offers to move everything into your Eternal stash *or* destroy it — take the stash move. *(Blizzard, both blogs)*
- **One direction only:** Eternal → Seasonal. There is no Seasonal → Eternal Rebirth, by definition. *(Blizzard season blog)*
- **Wording tightened between PTR and launch:** the PTR blog said cosmetics transfer outright; the launch blog adds *"when saved as part of an ensemble"*. Save your ensembles before pulling the trigger. *(Blizzard PTR blog vs season blog)* — if a note claims cosmetics always transfer, that claim is wrong.
- Icy Veins calls it "somewhat of a full reset, as any inventory items or currencies will be removed", cosmetics retained. *(Icy Veins season guide, Sep 15)*

## Horadric Post — one-way delivery channel

Account-level mailbox whose purpose is to deliver Blizzard-issued goods straight to your account: "The Horadric Post will occasionally contain **important deliveries carrying rewards, supplies, and commemorative items**." (Blizzard season blog, Sep 15; identical text in the PTR blog, Jul 30 — Icy Veins)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Direction | **One-way.** Icy Veins: "a blessed postal box straight from the ether" — there is no player-to-player sending. |
| Cadence | **"Occasionally"** — no fixed schedule published. |
| Contents | Blizzard-issued rewards, supplies, commemorative items. |
| Other restrictions | **None documented.** One-way delivery is the only stated property. |

- Because it is entirely team-driven, Icy Veins compares it to D3's post-season delivery and *speculates* it could carry Leaderboard rewards — *(speculation)*, no Blizzard statement. *(Icy Veins, Jul 30)*
- Practically: check it after patches, season-rank milestones and anniversary events; it is the delivery path for things no other system hands you. Rep-gated progression rewards are covered in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]].

## Legacy Uniques — all nine

Nine Uniques drawn from Diablo I, II and III, **permanent additions available on both Seasonal and Eternal realms**. Blizzard: "Legacy Uniques are typical Uniques in terms of power and utility" — original powers echoed, re-tuned to current itemization. (Blizzard season blog, Sep 15; PTR blog, Jul 30; permanence reconfirmed at the What's Next panel, Sep 13)

| Unique | Slot | Classes | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leoric's Crown** | Helm | All | Counts as Jewelry; increases the effect of any gem socketed into the helm by **75–100%** (D3 original applied to non-Legendary gems only) |
| **Nemesis Bracers** | Gloves | All | Shrines will spawn an Elite Pack |
| **Stone of Jordan** | Ring | All | Your Resistances are each equal to your highest, and you deal X more damage of that Element |
| **Squirt's Blouse** | Chest | All | When not taking Damage, you take up to **50%** and deal up to X more damage; bonus resets on losing life (Squirt's Necklace moved to a Chest) |
| **Arioc's Needle** | 2H Polearm | Barbarian, Druid, Spiritborn | Gain X Deadly Strike Chance; your Deadly Strikes deal X increased damage |
| **Henri's Perquisition** | Offhand Focus | Sorcerer, Necromancer, Warlock | You gain Primary Stat equal to X of your Dexterity |
| **In-Geom** | 1H Sword | Barbarian, Druid, Rogue, Necromancer, Paladin, Warlock | Defeating an Elite pack reduces your Skill Cooldowns by X seconds for **15 seconds** |
| **The Furnace** | 2H Mace | Barbarian, Druid, Necromancer, Paladin, Warlock | You deal X more damage to Elites |
| **Messerschmidt's Reaver** | 2H Axe | Barbarian, Druid, Necromancer, Paladin, Warlock | **Effect unlisted everywhere reachable** — Icy Veins still showed "TBD" at day 2. Left blank deliberately; do not backfill from RMT/boosting sites. The axe is "said to have been borne by Prince Aidan himself when he first drove back Diablo" |

Sources for the table: Icy Veins season-guide uniques table (Sep 15) + Blizzard season blog and PTR blog. *(Blizzard's own text lists Leoric's Crown as counting as "Jersey" — a typo for Jewelry; likewise the season blog heads its Twitch section "Season of **Hate's** Legacy".)*

**Live values revealed by the 3.2.1 PTR-bugfix list** — these confirm the items have real engine interactions and one number that changed: **In-Geom's cooldown reduction was 12s in the PTR and is 15s live**; Stone of Jordan had a Holy-damage interaction; Messerschmidt's Reaver had a class-availability bug; Leoric's Crown was not applying Soul Splinter effects, not enchantable, not unlocking its transmog on salvage and missing from Loot Filter options; Nemesis Bracers were not spawning elite packs from shrines; Squirt's Blouse stacks fell off randomly; Henri's Perquisition carried a stray Thorns affix. All fixed for launch. *(Blizzard, 3.2.1 patch notes, Sep 15)*

Build-level implications live in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Guide - Season 15 Tier List & Starters|Season 15 Tier List & Starters]]; Splinter synergies for Leoric's Crown in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]].

## Unique Charms and Mythic Charms

### 16 new Unique Charms

Sixteen Unique Charms, each based on an existing in-game Unique that previously had **no** Charm. (Blizzard 3.2.0 PTR patch notes, Talisman section)

**Ae'grom's Schism · Eye of Baal · Gospel of the Devotee · Grasp of Shadow · Hand of Apotheosis · Hellhound's Sabatons · Infernal Homunculus · Rictus of Terror · Shard of Verathiel · Signet of Pelghain · The Basilisk · The Hemat Stone · The Mortacrux · The Third Blade · Thundergod's Blessing · Vox Omnium**

### Legacy-Unique charm variants — 7, not 8

| | Legacy Uniques with a Charm | Legacy Uniques without |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | Nemesis Bracers, Stone of Jordan, Squirt's Blouse, Arioc's Needle, Henri's Perquisition, In-Geom, The Furnace (**7 of 9**) | **Leoric's Crown**, **Messerschmidt's Reaver** |

- **Flagged conflict:** the PTR blog said "**eight of the ten** legacy Uniques will also have Charms" — a pre-launch count written before the legacy list settled at nine. **The launch season blog's 7-of-9 list is the one to use**, and it matches the PTR patch notes' second Charm list. *(Blizzard PTR blog vs launch season blog vs PTR patch notes)*

### Mythic Unique Charms

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Drop rule | Any Unique Charm in the game has a **low chance to drop as a Mythic Unique Charm** — a Mythic roll is possible whenever a Unique Charm drops (Blizzard panel recap, Sep 13) |
| Stats | Properties roll at **maximum value** |
| Power | The Unique property is **significantly buffed** |
| Crafting | **None.** "There is no crafting method for Mythic Unique charms…" (Blizzard PTR blog, Jul 30) |
| Target farming | **None.** "…and like regular Unique Charms, there isn't a specific method for target farming." |

No drop-rate, pity-timer or cost numbers are published for Mythic Charms. Because there is no deterministic path, the only lever is Charm volume — see the Charm economy below and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Guide - Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy|Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy]].

### Talisman / Charm baseline, and the two new Cube recipes

- **Slots come from Seals:** Magic 3 / Rare 4 / Legendary 5 / **Mythic Unique 6**; **Mythic Unique Seals drop only in the highest Torment tiers**. Charms are Magic/Rare, Set or Unique. Charms and Seals salvage at the Occultist into **Infused Horadric Resin**; 3-to-1 Transmutation and Reroll Set Charm are the legacy crafting options. This is the **pre-S15 Lord of Hatred baseline** (Maxroll reference, last updated Jun 28, 2026) — it predates Mythic Charms and the new recipes.
- **Affix Value Reroll** (new): rerolls all base affixes on equipment **and Talisman items (Charms and Seals)** while **protecting Greater Affixes, Enchanted affixes, Tempers and Transfigurations** — unless the item is Unmodifiable. (Blizzard season blog, Sep 15)
- **Horadric Reroll** (new): a second Charm/Seal option for **Legendary+** Charms and Seals; fully randomises base affixes **without touching Set Bonuses or Unique/Mythic charm powers**, and unlike Affix Value Reroll it will not change Charm Set Bonus affixes on Seals. Icy Veins describes it as rerolling "Legendary and **Mythic** stats" where Blizzard says "Legendary+" — **the two wordings disagree**; treat Blizzard's as authoritative pending in-game confirmation. *(Blizzard season blog + Maxroll; Icy Veins day 2)*

## Rune Word Crafting — shadow-dropped at launch, not in the patch notes

Season 15 shipped with a **Rune Word Crafting** system plus new Uniques and Runes that "Blizzard has quietly shadow dropped" — it appears **nowhere in the 3.2.1 patch notes** and in no Blizzard source read. Runewords work like Diablo II: combine a **common-quality (white) base item with specific Runes in the Horadric Cube** to produce a Unique. *(community-consensus, day 2 — Icy Veins, Sep 15)*

## What's Next — the parts that land in S15 vs later seasons

**Live in S15 now:**
- **Shared War Plans** — account-wide War Plans reputation across alts in the same partition (Eternal/Seasonal, Non-Hardcore/Hardcore, Normal/SSF); characters share rep levels but each allocates its own points, like Paragon. S14 War Plans progress carried into Eternal at the Sep 15 season change. Full detail in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]]. *(Blizzard season blog + panel recap, Sep 13)*
- Everything documented on this page: Mythic/Iconic crafting changes, the two new Cube recipes, Mythic Charms, legacy Uniques, Rebirth, Horadric Post.

**Not S15 — future seasons or later products. Listed only so it is not mistaken for current content:**
- Further War Plans refinements (playlist customisation, Seasonal variety) and itemization/customisation expansion for new build variety — future seasons *(Blizzard panel recap)*.
- **The Amazon** arrives as a class pack in **H1 2027**, with four archetypes: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, Commander *(Blizzard panel recap)*.
- **Diablo V — Spring 2029** *(Blizzard panel recap)*.
- A **Netflix animated series** is in development *(Blizzard panel recap)*.
- **Story arc continuity:** S15 carries the S14 "Season of Death Awakening" storyline forward rather than resetting — Blizzard confirms "Season of Hell's Legacy and upcoming Seasons carry on the Season of Death's Awakening story"; Rhykker reads the panel as the team "testing out a seasonal arc" *(confirmed for continuity, community-consensus for the framing)*.

## Open blanks worth remembering

- **Messerschmidt's Reaver's effect is unpublished** — Icy Veins still had it as TBD at day 2 and no Blizzard text states it. Blank, not "unknown, probably X".
- **Runeword list is datamined and incomplete** — no official list, no in-patch-notes entry. Re-check in ~2 weeks.
- **Mythic Charms have no crafting and no target farming** — that is a design statement, not a gap in the research.
- **Horadric Post cadence** is "occasionally"; no schedule, no other restrictions documented.
- **Rebirth and currencies:** Blizzard's copy says gear/items are lost; Icy Veins adds currencies. The exact currency treatment is only in the secondary source — verify if it matters to you.
- **Prime Evil encounter permanence is not confirmed** — the legacy Uniques and Unique Charms *are* explicitly permanent; the Diablo/Baal/Mephisto fights and the Prime Evil rep track are S15-seasonal until Blizzard says otherwise *(speculation)*.
