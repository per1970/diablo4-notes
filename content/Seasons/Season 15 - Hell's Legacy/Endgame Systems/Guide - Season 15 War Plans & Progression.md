> [!d4-s15] War Plans & Endgame Progression
> War Plans reputation/progress are now **account-wide but partitioned** · T10+ node XP orbs **2 → 4**, XP-reward-node orbs doubled again · Pit progress-orb XP **doubled** with Choron's Soul, T150 bonus **1800% → 2700%** · Whisper caches ~3× gold, **2× Tributes / 5× Sigil Dust** on key caches · **no node, tree or Tower redesign anywhere in 3.2.1**

## War Plans — what changed in 3.2.1

| | S14 | S15 / 3.2.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Reputation & progress | Per-character | **Shared account-wide**, partitioned |
| Planner / point allocation | Per-character | Per-character (**unchanged**) |
| Baseline XP orbs per completed node, T10+ | 2 | **4** — all reward types and rarities |
| Orbs from XP reward nodes (Magic/Rare/Legendary/Mythic) | baseline | **Doubled again** on top of the baseline |
| Structure | ≤5 chained activities → War Chest (Temis); 7-node permanent tree per activity | **Unchanged** |
| Node names, costs, respec values | — | **Unchanged** |

*(Blizzard 3.2.1 notes; Maxroll season guide, Sep 13; Icy Veins)* — `confirmed`

The account-wide change is the headline: **War Plans reputation and progress are shared across all characters on the same account, partitioned by Seasonal/Eternal × Hardcore/non-Hardcore × SSF/non-SSF.** Each character still keeps its own planner and its own point allocation — what is shared is the earned reputation/progress pool inside that partition, not the spend. (Maxroll season guide, Sep 13, quoting Blizzard; Icy Veins, same announcement text)

### Carryover and the new Hardcore rule

- **S14 War Plans progress carried over to Eternal at the September 15 changeover.** (Maxroll, Sep 13)
- **New Hardcore rule:** only *living* S14 Hardcore characters transfer their points. Transferring a dead Hardcore character's points requires **one living Hardcore character in the same partition** to run the changeover — a level-1 "storage" character is explicitly allowed. (Maxroll, Sep 13; Icy Veins)
- SSF partitions exist in the scheme even though SSF was disabled at launch on September 15 after login-blocking issues — see [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/index|Season 15 Index]].

### No structure redesign — what that means for your S13/S14 knowledge

**3.2.1's War Plans section contains only XP tuning and bug fixes.** Nothing renamed, re-costed or re-pathed. The fixes listed are: teleport cancelling skill animations, Waking Spoils chests lootable without the node, Duriel's Invasion persisting after its enabler left, Belial ambush loot, Nemesis Tide outdated materials, and repeatable Ultimate Nemesis Lair farming. (Blizzard 3.2.1 notes, Vitablo / Sportskeeda) — `confirmed` for "no node changes"

Consequence: **S13/S14 node knowledge still holds structurally, but XP nodes are now relatively better.** 3.2.1 doubled XP-node orb output while nerfing Helltide maggot orbs, so every XP-reward node and XP node — Undercity's **Jade Epiphany**, Helltide's **Writhe and Rot(e)** — is worth relatively more than it was. No post-3.2.1 node-value re-review has been published yet, so the *re-ranking itself* is `speculation` (node identity is `community-consensus`).

## Endgame activity priority — first week

**Which activity you farm first is downstream of your Splinter of Evil pick** — the Splinter sets the amplified activity family: (MP1st, Sep 15, summarising Blizzard's seasonal breakdown; Maxroll, Sep 13) — `confirmed`

| Splinter | Amplified activity family |
|---|---|
| **Diablo** — Splinter of Terror | Helltide + Infernal Hordes |
| **Baal** — Splinter of Destruction | Kurast Undercity + Lair Bosses |
| **Mephisto** — Splinter of Hatred | The Pit + Nightmare Dungeons |

All three also affect the Tree of Whispers. Full mechanics, swap cost and the Splinter-of-Evil vs Soul-Splinter naming trap: [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes|Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes]] · [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]].

### Practical order

1. **Unlock Torment** (endgame capstone dungeon — the hardest day-1 gate; Torment unlocks Greater Lair Boss runs, Mythic drops, Resplendent Sparks).
2. **Pit runs** for Glyph upgrades + Masterworking materials — **the Pit is the only Glyph-XP source**, so this step is mandatory regardless of build. See [[Mechanics/Nightmare Dungeon Affixes|Nightmare Dungeon Affixes]] for the sigil side.
3. **Pick the Splinter that matches the farm** you just chose — Mephisto for glyph/Obducite, Baal for boss targets, Diablo for Helltide/Hordes.
4. **Whispers as the concurrent reward layer** — 3.2.1 buffed caches hard (roughly 3× gold, doubled gem fragments, 2× Tributes on key caches).
5. **Undercity** for Mythics / XP / Forgotten Souls. **Lair Bosses** for targeted Uniques. **Nightmare Dungeons** for Goblin loot and Escalation Sigils.
6. **Waking Nightmares** are a targeted loot + Splinter-swap loop, **not** an XP farm — the hardest versions award the resource that swaps your Prime Evil and changes reputation track. Pinnacle Capstone versions sit at Torment 10+. [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Waking Nightmares|Season 15 Waking Nightmares]]

### Activity tiering — treat the S13 table as stale

The S13 vault ranking was Helltide **S**, Undercity **S**, Whispers **S** (passive), Lair Bosses **A**, Nightmare Dungeons **A**, The Pit **B**, Infernal Hordes **D — avoid**. **That ranking predates 3.2.1's Pit XP doubling and the new Challenge Dungeons, and should be re-derived rather than copied.** A flat "Pit = B" no longer describes the XP use case. `community-consensus` (day 2, will move)

- **Challenge Dungeons (new in S15)** are reported as the fastest bulk Paragon-XP route **but award no Glyph XP**, leaving the Pit mandatory. Reported advice: push ~3 Torment tiers above your gear for reward density; a ~30-second overland Ambush event can spawn between portals. `low-trust` — timesaver.gg, Sep 11. *Not corroborated by a high-reliability source this sweep: the dungeons exist (PTR-previewed, confirmed), the XP comparison is not.*
- **Paragon continues to 300**, with ~200 as Blizzard's stated "fully realized build" design target. `community-consensus` — quoted from a Blizzard developer note via timesaver.gg.

## The Pit in S15

- **Experience from Progress Orbs is DOUBLED while the Choron's Soul Pit node is active.** (Blizzard 3.2.1 notes, "The Pit" subsection)
- **Higher-tier bonus XP increased — Pit Tier 150 example: 1800% → 2700%.**
- Bug fixes: Pit Guardian Protective Barrier fixed; Pit Guardians no longer mis-spawn when party members transition floors.

`confirmed` — Vitablo (Sep 12) + Sportskeeda (Sep 14), independent reproductions of the same notes text.

Separately, both the Pit and the Tower carry leaderboards, but only the Tower's are described as the *official* in-game ranked boards — `speculation`, third-party blog, undated excerpt.

## What's not settled

- **Provenance of the 3.2.1 numbers.** Blizzard's own patch-notes page returned only up to **3.1.3 (Aug 12, 2026)** when crawled — no 3.2.1 section. Every number above comes from two independent reproductions of the same notes text (Vitablo, Sportskeeda) plus MP1st's summary, which agree with each other. Internally consistent, but none came from Blizzard's own page in this sweep.
- **Tower silence is meaningful, not proof.** Zero Tower entries in 3.2.1 reads as "unchanged from S14"; it does not confirm the S15 weekly cadence or reward tiers.
- **The vault's own S13 activity tiering is stale** (see above) — 3.2.1's Pit XP doubling and the Challenge Dungeons addition undermine a flat "Pit = B".
- **Two low-trust sources are load-bearing for the day-order skeleton:** timesaver.gg (boosting/RMT site — sells gold, power leveling, boss carries) and AOEAH (RMT site). Their claims are tagged inline; AOEAH independently lists account-wide War Plans and the Helltide maggot XP adjustment (both corroborated), plus a "reworked mythic drop sources" claim and tree levels 1 → 7 that are **low-trust only**.
- **Day-2 volatility.** All XP-efficiency and ranking claims will move. Re-check nodes and routes in ~2 weeks, once wudijo/Maxroll publish post-launch War Plans and endgame coverage.
