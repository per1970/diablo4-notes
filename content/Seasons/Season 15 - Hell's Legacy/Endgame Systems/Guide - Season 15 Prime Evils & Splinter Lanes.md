> [!d4-s15] Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes
> **Splinters of Evil** (3 Prime Evil picks → seasonal reputation track) ≠ **Soul Splinters** (8 socketable jewelry gems) — near-identical names, unrelated systems · your pick amplifies one activity family: Diablo→Helltide + Infernal Hordes, Baal→Undercity + Lair Bosses, Mephisto→Pit + Nightmare Dungeons, all three→Tree of Whispers · swappable, not permanent — the swap resource drops from the largest Waking Nightmares · day-2 data, rep ranks will move

## Read me first: two unrelated systems are both called "Splinter"

Blizzard shipped two S15 systems with near-identical names. Keep them apart — this note covers only the first; the gems live in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]].

| | **Splinters of Evil** (this note) | **Soul Splinters** (gems) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | 3 Prime Evil picks — Diablo / Baal / Mephisto | 8 socketable gems (Andariel, Azmodan, Belial, Black Soulstone, Duriel, Lilith, Na-Krul, Skarn) |
| Equipped | one at a time, on your character | up to 3, jewelry slots only |
| Does | drives the seasonal reputation track + War Plan modifiers | stats/resistances + one signature effect with a drawback |
| Changing it | swap requires a rare resource from the largest Waking Nightmares | free — they're gems |

(Blizzard season blog, Sept 15 · Icy Veins, day 2). Both systems are `confirmed` as existing and as separate; everything downstream of the pick in this note is day-2 community measurement. Hub: [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/index|Season 15 Index]].

## The three Prime Evil encounters

All three Prime Evils return as **Echoes** in the Seasonal Questline, at three recreated locations, and **defeating them is what grants the three Splinters** — you then choose one to bear. (Blizzard season blog, Sept 15)

| Prime Evil | Splinter | Iconic Power element | Encounter location | Amplifies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diablo (Lord of Terror) | Splinter of **Terror** | Fire | Beneath **Tristram Cathedral** | Helltide + Infernal Hordes |
| Baal (Lord of Destruction) | Splinter of **Destruction** | Cold | **Arreat Summit** *(launch blog)* / **Worldstone Chamber** *(panel)* — see conflict | Kurast Undercity + Lair Bosses |
| Mephisto (Lord of Hatred) | Splinter of **Hatred** | Physical | **Durance of Hate** | The Pit + Nightmare Dungeons |

- **All three tracks also modify the Tree of Whispers.** Attribution for the mapping: Blizzard's launch blog, independently restated by Icy Veins and MP1st. (Blizzard season blog · Icy Veins, day 2 · MP1st, Sept 15)
- **Baal's location is a live source conflict.** The Blizzard launch blog says **Arreat Summit**; the BlizzCon panel recap says the three thrones are "Worldstone Chamber, the Durance of Hate, and the original Diablo's Tristram Cathedral", and Rhykker describes the Baal fight as the end of D2 Act V "right next to the World Stone". Arreat Summit is the room you cross immediately before the Worldstone Chamber, so both may be literally true — **treat the campaign as ending at the Worldstone.** Do not silently pick one. (Blizzard season blog vs Blizzard panel recap vs Rhykker, Sept 13)
- Diablo specifically: you fight an Echo of him under Tristram Cathedral in the questline **and** again in the Diablo-themed Waking Nightmare capstone. Rhykker: "fight your way down through Tristram Cathedral to tackle big boy Diablo himself". (Blizzard season blog · Rhykker, Sept 13)
- **Related 30th-anniversary event, separate from these encounters:** "Moophisto" / the Divine Bovine community challenge. Confirmed as an official Blizzard article title; **mechanics unread — do not fill them in.** `speculation`
- Separate again: the **Overland Ambush** (a demon hunts you in the open world, 30-second warning) is *not* part of the splinter system, despite the "Splinter of the Mother" name overlap. See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Reference - Season 15 New Systems|Season 15 New Systems]].

## Bearing a Splinter — what you actually get

While bearing one you become a **dark wanderer**, and an **Iconic Power** of that Prime Evil periodically erupts in combat (**Diablo = Fire, Baal = Cold, Mephisto = Physical**). (Blizzard season blog)

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Rep track size | **8 activity modifiers + 6 upgrades to the Iconic Power**, per Prime Evil |
| Both tracks | All three modify the Tree of Whispers nodes as well as their own two activities |
| Cost of bearing it | You become **more vulnerable to the Prime Evil you bear** ("what you hunt will stalk you in return") |
| Ambush mechanic | Bearing a splinter **gets you randomly ambushed by an echo of that Prime Evil** (Rhykker-confirmed) |
| Rank language | UI "splinter rank" means **season reputation**, not a splinter rank-up system *(wudijo explicit correction)* |

(Blizzard season blog · Rhykker, Sept 13 · wudijo, Sept 16)

**Swappable, not permanent, and not free.** Splinters can be changed; the rare resource that allows a swap drops from the **largest Waking Nightmares** (the multi-level Capstone-type dungeons). See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Waking Nightmares|Season 15 Waking Nightmares]]. Cliptis reports the first swap is gated behind **Torment 6** *(community-consensus — he had not confirmed it himself)*. Neither the swap-resource drop rate nor a swap limit is documented by Blizzard.

## Reputation tracks: the named War Plan nodes and their rank gates

Each Prime Evil's track gates specific **War Plan** nodes, so the pick changes which seasonal bonuses are even reachable. Day-2 node names and rep ranks (Icy Veins, day 2) — **`community-consensus`, expect retuning**; ranks are early-season and will shift. Node mechanics background: [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]].

| Lane | Node | Rep gate | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helltide (Terror) | Wretched Vermin | Terror r4 | — |
| Helltide (Terror) | Hellmouth | Terror r12 | Hellwyrms can summon a Butcher |
| Helltide (Terror) | Tainted Shrines | Terror r18 | — |
| Infernal Hordes (Terror) | — | — | *no named nodes surfaced in the day-2 list* |
| Undercity (Destruction) | Jade Epiphany | Destruction r4 | **+50% Experience Orbs, +1 Monster Power** |
| Undercity (Destruction) | Endless Swarm | Destruction r8 | Invasion Portal at the start of each floor |
| Undercity (Destruction) | Last Gasp | Destruction r12 | Double time earned at ≤15s instead of ≤5s |
| NMD (Hatred) | Waking Spoils | Hatred r4 | 50% chance of a second War's Bounty Chest |
| NMD (Hatred) | Dread Revival | Hatred r8 | — |
| NMD (Hatred) | Horadric Hunter | Hatred r12 | — |
| Tree of Whispers (all) | Corrupted Roots / Roots of Power | Terror r8 / **r22** | Roots of Power: **+1 Monster Power** and a large Aberrant Cinder yield in Helltide |
| Tree of Whispers (all) | Wisdom of Whispers / Resplendent Favor | Hatred r5 / **r22** | Resplendent Favor: Whisper Caches can drop **2 Resplendent Sparks instead of 1** |
| Tree of Whispers (all) | Grim Retribution / Colossal Foe | Destruction r5 / **r22** | Colossal Foe: **+2 Monster Power** and Superior Lair Keys |

The **r22 capstone on the Tree of Whispers** is the deepest reachable node listed per track — Terror buys cinders, Hatred buys Sparks, Destruction buys Lair Keys and Monster Power. If you only pick one lane, that's the bonus you never get.

## How the pick drives endgame selection

The activity families are not equivalent in S15, so the lane choice is effectively a farming-plan choice. 3.2.1 retunes below are from the patch notes as reproduced by Vitablo (Sept 12) and Sportskeeda (Sept 14) — Blizzard's own patch-notes page was still serving 3.1.3 during the sweep, so numbers come from reproductions.

| Lane | Activities it amplifies | What that activity is actually for in S15 | 3.2.1 movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Destruction (Baal) | Kurast Undercity + Lair Bosses | Undercity = current best **leveling** route and Mythic/XP/Forgotten-Soul source; Lair Bosses = targeted Uniques | Undercity **Jade Epiphany orbs doubled**; Forgotten Souls added to Tribute rewards |
| Hatred (Mephisto) | The Pit + Nightmare Dungeons | **Pit is the only Glyph-XP source**, so it's mandatory regardless; NMD for Goblin loot and Escalation Sigils | Pit Progress-Orb XP **doubled** with Choron's Soul; higher-tier bonus XP up (**T150: 1800% → 2700%**) |
| Terror (Diablo) | Helltide + Infernal Hordes | Both just nerfed / unpopular; wudijo: Hordes and Helltide are things "almost no one really does" | Helltide **nerf**: Hellmouth maggot orbs reduced, Portent of Pain and Plague Devourers now drop **1–2 orbs instead of always 2** |
| All three | Tree of Whispers | The concurrent reward layer on every route — turn in at 10 Grim Favors | Large **buff**: cache orbs **3–5 → 4–6** (+2–4 at Torment X+); Gold Cache ≈3× (**≈35M → ≈100M** at T12); key caches 2× Tributes, 5× Sigil Dust, small chance of a Mephisto Key |

Two lane-specific endgame facts worth holding: **Echo of Mephisto now has a guaranteed Mythic drop** (3.2.1), which props up the Destruction/Lair-Boss lane; and the Pit has no alternative for glyph upgrades, so the Hatred lane's headline activity is in every first-week plan whether or not you bear Mephisto. Activity tiering inside the vault predates 3.2.1's Pit XP doubling and should be re-derived, not copied. ## Creator recommendation order (day 2)

**wudijo — Baal first, then Mephisto. Diablo is the weak pick.** (wudijo, "D4 Season 15 BEST SPLINTER CHOICES", Sept 16)

1. **Baal / Destruction — take first.** Boosts Undercity, which is the current best leveling strategy, and grants a **50% XP bonus** among its seasonal bonuses. He rates it best for leveling and alts.
2. **Mephisto / Hatred — take second.** Targets the Pit and Nightmare Dungeons: more glyph upgrades, and the run is treated **"as if you were 10 tiers higher"**, which makes **glyph leveling to 150 feasible on weaker builds**. Best for glyph/Paragon farmers and low-ceiling archetypes; wudijo expects "pit meta this time again" because Helltide/worm Paragon farming was nerfed.
3. **Diablo / Terror — weak.** Feeds Infernal Hordes and Helltides, both nerfed and both thinly played. Only worth it if you specifically live in Helltide.
4. wudijo also stresses that each splinter feeds specific War Plan nodes, so the choice changes which seasonal bonuses are reachable at all — see the node table above.

**Cliptis — same content map, with the swappability caveat:** pick by content, the choice is locked for a while after the questline and can be changed later (his reported Torment-6 gate). (Cliptis, Sept 16)

**Rob2628 — a build-selection side effect:** single-target DPS matters more this season because the Prime Evils return as bosses ("it seems like single target DPS this season is very important with these new bosses"), which is part of why he rates Warlock first. This bumps boss-optimised archetypes over pure AoE speed builds. `speculation` — single source, stated as his own expectation, encounter frequency unknown. (Rob2628, Sept 15) Class-level picture: [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Guide - Season 15 Tier List & Starters|Season 15 Tier List & Starters]]

**Conflict to note:** day-2 sources describe the lock as temporary and swappable, but only Cliptis reports a concrete gate value (Torment 6) and he flags it as unverified. Blizzard documents only the swap *resource*, not a gate.

## Is any of this permanent?

**Blizzard does not say Diablo is permanent.** The launch blog explicitly labels the **legacy Uniques** a permanent addition to Seasonal *and* Eternal; there is **no equivalent statement** for the Prime Evil Echo encounters, the Splinter-of-Evil reputation track, or the Waking Nightmares. Icy Veins frames Diablo's appearance as "in a slightly informal format due to it being an anniversary event". `speculation`

**Vault stance: treat the Prime Evil encounters and the splinter-of-Evil rep track as S15-seasonal until Blizzard says otherwise.** The legacy Uniques and Unique Charms are the parts actually confirmed permanent. (Blizzard season blog's silence + Icy Veins season guide, day 2)
