> [!d4-s15] Waking Nightmares — Three Types, ~1-Minute Portals, Thin Documentation
> Three encounter types: multi-level Capstone · fixed-location standard dungeon · sudden one-room overworld portal · portals live **~1 minute** and spawn most often in **Helltide** · the largest award the rare resource that swaps your **Splinter of Evil** · launch difficulty selection is via **in-room braziers**, *not* the PTR's "up to three Torment tiers above your rank" · **the least-documented system of Season 15** — day-2 data only

## What it is

Waking Nightmares are rebuilt fights from Diablo I, Hellfire, II, III and Immortal, with updated visuals, folded into the seasonal endgame as **new content that sits alongside** Helltides, the Pit, Undercity and Lair Bosses (`confidence: confirmed` — Blizzard News 3.2.1 season blog, 2026-09-15; restated by the What's Next panel recap, 2026-09-13).

Blizzard frames them as an **addition**, never as a replacement for any existing loop — including SSF. Blizzard does not address SSF and Waking Nightmares in the same sentence at all; the "addition, not replacement" reading is inference from framing, not a Blizzard statement (`confidence: speculation` — nothing in either source claims replacement). SSF's continued absence at day 2 is an unrelated launch defect (hotfix 1, September 15; see [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]]).

## The three types

| Type | How you enter | Duration | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Multi-level "Capstone"-style dungeon** (largest) — recreates a historic Prime Evil showdown | First met in the **Seasonal Questline**; replayable afterwards **on Torment levels at a fixed difficulty**, like all Capstones. Also available as **Pinnacle Capstones at Torment 10+**, for greater risk and bigger loot | Multi-level; per-run length not published | The **most difficult** ones award the **rare resource that lets you switch which Splinter of Evil you bear** (the rep-track respec mechanism). Everything else is standard loot — **no reward table, no drop rate published** |
| **Standard-length dungeon** at a **fixed world location**, like a normal dungeon (Blizzard's example: the **Den of Evil**, Diablo II) | Walk to its world location and enter, as with any normal dungeon | Standard dungeon length | Standard loot; **entirely unspecified** |
| **Sudden open-world one-room encounter** with a past boss (e.g. the giant spider **Queen Araneae**) or waves of classic monsters (dagger-wielding **Flayers**) | A **portal opens in the Overworld while you are farming**; it stays open roughly **one minute** before disappearing. **Helltide is the most common place to find them**, because that is where players farm the overworld | One room, one fight; the entry window (~1 min) is the real clock | Standard loot; **unspecified** |

Sources: types and Capstone/Pinnacle framing — Blizzard season blog (2026-09-15) + What's Next panel recap (2026-09-13); portal lifetime, braziers and Helltide frequency — `community-consensus (day 2)`, Icy Veins season guide (updated Sept 15, 2026 15:53); Splinter-swap resource — Blizzard panel recap + season blog (`confirmed`).

### Pinnacle Capstones

The Pinnacle variant at **Torment 10+** is the only stated escalation path for the largest Waking Nightmares. Neither source states whether Pinnacles are replayable on demand, whether they share the fixed-difficulty Capstone rule, or what "bigger loot" means numerically (`confidence: speculation` — Blizzard names the tier gate, not the mechanics).

## Overworld portal behaviour

- Portals appear **during overworld farming** and close after roughly **one minute** (`community-consensus (day 2)` — Icy Veins).
- Inside is a **small arena**; the room has **multiple braziers around it**, and interacting with braziers **raises the encounter's difficulty before the fight starts** — the difficulty choice happens in the room, not at a pre-entry selector (`community-consensus (day 2)` — Icy Veins).
- **Helltide is the common spawn point**, but that is a function of where players farm, not a documented spawn restriction.
- Terminology trap for a vault grep: Blizzard's own 3.2.1 bug-fix list says "**Wakening** Nightmare portals" while every other page says Waking Nightmares (`confirmed` — the typo exists in the patch notes).

## Difficulty — an unresolved conflict

Two Blizzard pages describe difficulty in incompatible terms, and the launch-state third-party description follows neither:

- **PTR blog (3.2.0, 2026-07-30), verbatim:** "In Torment difficulties, you'll be able to choose how difficult you want the fight to be, **up to three Torment tiers above your current difficulty**." That text sat under the heading **"Challenge Dungeons"** (`confidence: confirmed` that the PTR text says this).
- **Launch season blog (3.2.1, 2026-09-15):** never repeats that phrasing. It describes **fixed-difficulty Capstones** plus **Pinnacle Capstones gated at Torment 10+** (`confirmed` — the phrasing is absent from the launch text).
- **Icy Veins (day 2):** difficulty is raised by **in-room braziers**, not a pre-entry tier selector (`community-consensus (day 2)`).

**Treat "up to three Torment tiers above your rank" as PTR-documented and launch-unconfirmed.** Do not present it as a live rule. The launch mechanic could be braziers only, braziers *plus* a Pinnacle gate, or something re-tuned since — no source read resolves it, and no dedicated Waking Nightmares guide exists anywhere reachable at day 2 (see [What is still undocumented](#what-is-still-undocumented)).

## Rewards and the Splinter swap

The headline reward is the **rare resource that lets you change which Splinter of Evil you bear** — Waking Nightmares are the respec mechanism for the seasonal reputation pick, and the largest/hardest ones are where it drops (`confidence: confirmed` — Blizzard panel recap + season blog). Which Splinter you carry decides which activity family your War Plan nodes amplify, so this is the practical reason to run the big ones: see [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes|Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes]] for the per-Prime-Evil node mapping, and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 War Plans & Progression|Season 15 War Plans & Progression]] for how the rep tracks gate those nodes.

Everything else they drop is described by Blizzard only as "rewards". **No item list, no drop table, no drop rate, and no published rate for the Splinter-swap resource itself.** Do not backfill these from RMT/boosting sites.

## Roster and callbacks

- **Nearly 20 bosses return**, including **Nihlathak** and the **Maggot Queen** (`confirmed` — Blizzard panel recap; corroborated by Rhykker and a `low-trust` RMT-adjacent piece read for cross-reference only).
- **Three new Capstone dungeons, one per Prime Evil** (`community-consensus` — Rhykker, 2026-09-13).
- Named locations confirmed: **Den of Evil** (fixed-location dungeon example), **Queen Araneae**, **Flayers**.
- Friendly-NPC Easter eggs: **Deckard Cain** returning as a lost soul with new lines from the original voice actor, and **Tyrael** in his classic Diablo II appearance (`community-consensus` — Icy Veins + Rhykker; Blizzard confirms Cain as quest giver).

## What is still undocumented

State these as blanks. The digest's access log records that **no dedicated Waking Nightmares mechanics or reward guide exists on Maxroll, Icy Veins' news hub, or anywhere else reachable at day 2** — the only descriptions are summary paragraphs inside the season guides. Applying that:

- **Difficulty scaling numbers.** Exact Torment tiers offered per encounter, the brazier difficulty steps (how many braziers, how large each step is), and whether the PTR's three-tier figure survives in any form. **Unresolved conflict of record** — PTR blog vs launch blog vs Icy Veins.
- **Reward tables and drop rates.** No published loot list for any of the three types, and **no published drop rate for the Splinter-swap resource** — including whether it is guaranteed from the hardest Pinnacle Capstone.
- **Duration and length.** No stated run length for the multi-level Capstones or the fixed-location dungeons; only the ~1-minute portal window is quantified.
- **Portal spawn rates and conditions.** No spawn frequency, no spawn-region list beyond "Helltide is commonest", no statement on whether a portal can be missed or re-rolled.
- **Structure of the fixed-location dungeons.** No list of locations, no statement of how many exist, no unique affixes, no sigil/Talisman interaction.
- **Group and scaling behaviour.** Nothing on party scaling, Monster Power interaction, or whether these count as Nightmare Dungeons for effects/objectives that reference them (compare [[Mechanics/Nightmare Dungeon Affixes|Nightmare Dungeon Affixes]] — no source states an affix relationship).
- **Pinnacle Capstone mechanics.** Only the Torment 10+ gate is documented, not replayability, loot delta, or difficulty selection.
- **Permanence.** Blizzard explicitly labels the **legacy Uniques** and **Unique Charms** as permanent additions to Seasonal *and* Eternal, and makes **no equivalent statement** about Waking Nightmares or the Prime Evil encounters. Icy Veins frames Diablo's appearance as "in a slightly informal format due to it being an anniversary event". Treat Waking Nightmares as **S15-seasonal until Blizzard says otherwise** (`confidence: speculation` — Blizzard's silence plus one third-party reading).
- **Post-launch re-tuning.** Day-2 data only; Blizzard has already shown it will change numbers after launch (SSF disabled, Rune Word Crafting shadow-dropped unannounced). Refresh in ~2 weeks.
