> [!d4-s15] Soul Splinters
> Eight socketable jewelry gems, max 3 equipped · every one of them carries a real drawback · same-type + same-rarity amalgamates to the next tier in the Horadric Cube · per-step cost is **not published anywhere** · rarity count conflicts: 5 tiers (PTR blog) vs 4 (launch blog)

## Naming trap: two unrelated "Splinter" systems

Blizzard shipped two systems in S15 whose names collide. Getting them mixed up is the single easiest mistake to make with this note:

| System | What it is | Slots |
|---|---|---|
| **Splinters of Evil** | The three Prime Evil picks — Diablo / Baal / Mephisto. They drive the seasonal reputation track and which War Plan nodes you can reach. Acquired by finishing the Seasonal Questline, swappable later via the rare resource from the hardest Waking Nightmares. | 1 equipped |
| **Soul Splinters** | The eight socketable gems this note covers (Andariel, Azmodan, Belial, Black Soulstone, Duriel, Lilith, Na-Krul, Skarn). | Up to 3, jewelry only |

Lane mapping, War Plan node gates and rep-track detail live in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Guide - Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes|Season 15 Prime Evils & Splinter Lanes]] — this note deliberately does not duplicate it.

**On "a splinter is an activity selector, not flat power":** that framing is correct but belongs to the *Splinter of Evil* pick. Baal converts your seasonal bonuses into Undercity/Lair Boss rewards, Mephisto into Pit/Nightmare Dungeon rewards, Diablo into Helltide/Infernal Hordes (all three touch the Tree of Whispers) — so the Prime Evil is a content choice. Day-2 creator coverage (Cliptis, wudijo, Sep 16) uses "splinter" loosely across both systems; when a source says "pick by content" it means the Prime Evil. **No source maps any of the eight Soul Splinters to a specific activity** (Blizzard season blog, Sept 15; Icy Veins, day 2). The real Soul-Splinter decision is *which drawback your build can absorb*, not which content you want to run.

## The eight Soul Splinters

Values are the PTR/live ladders as published; where a ladder runs four steps, read it as tier 1→4 (see the rarity conflict below). Intrinsic resistances match the normal gem they replace.

| Soul Splinter | Prime Evil | Resistance | Boon | Drawback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Anguish** | Andariel | Fire Resistance | +15/20/25/35% Critical Strike Chance **and** Attack Speed | Primary Resource Costs **+30%** |
| **Sin** | Azmodan | Lightning Resistance | +10/20/30/40%[x] Maximum Health | Maximum Primary Resource **−30%** |
| **Lies** | Belial | Cold Resistance | +40/60/80/100% Gold and Crafting Material Drop Rate, **+25% Bonus Experience** | **Permanently Weakened and Vulnerable** |
| **the Black Soulstone** | *(none named)* | *not listed* | +7/20/30/50 All Stats; killing enemies absorbs a soul for 15s, each soul **+2/3/4/5%[x] damage** | **+0.5% damage taken per soul** — stacks, does not refresh |
| **Pain** | Duriel | Poison Resistance | +10/20/30/40% Damage Reduction | **−30% Movement Speed** |
| **the Mother** | Lilith | Physical Resistance | Unhindered; touching enemies applies Vulnerable for 10s | After touching 100/150/200/250 enemies you are **Stunned for 4 seconds** |
| **Hellfire** | Na-Krul | 35/50/375/625 Resistance to All Elements | **+40/60/80/100%[x] damage** | **−100% Critical Strike Chance** |
| **Damnation** | Skarn | Shadow Resistance | Killing an Elite Pack increases **Monster Power by 1** for 10/15/20/25 seconds | The same effect — a self-imposed difficulty escalator, not a debuff you can play around |

*(Blizzard 3.2.0 PTR blog, Jul 29–30, 2026, per-tier values; Icy Veins, Jul 30, 2026, full table + drawback list. Blizzard's launch blog confirms the eight names and the drawback structure but does not reprint the ladders.)*

Notes on the data:
- The Hellfire resistance ladder reads `35/50/375/625` as published (Icy Veins, Jul 30). The 375 step looks transposed, but no source corrects it — left verbatim rather than silently repaired.
- Two drawbacks are flat rather than tiered: Anguish's +30% resource cost and Pain's −30% movement speed. They do not get better with a higher-rarity splinter.
- The Black Soulstone splinter is the only entry with **no elemental resistance listed** and **no Prime Evil association** — the odd one out in its own table, and the one whose drawback scales without a cap.
- Hellfire is the only splinter whose drawback is a *hard negation* (−100% crit chance). On a build that does not crit anyway it is close to free; on everything else it deletes a stat line.

## Equipping: max three, jewelry only, and Leoric's Crown

- **Up to three equipped, jewelry slots only** (Blizzard season blog, Sept 15). They are framed as "special gems", so they compete with normal gems rather than adding a socket type.
- **Consequence flagged by Icy Veins (Sept 15):** because they carry the normal elemental resistances, Soul Splinters are expected to *replace* normal gems in jewelry rather than stack with them — so the trade is a resistance socket plus a drawback for the signature effect.
- **Leoric's Crown interaction:** the Crown counts as Jewelry *and* increases the effect of any gem socketed into the helm by 75–100%, so it can host a Soul Splinter and amplify it. Icy Veins warns this risks repeating the Heir of Perdition problem (every build wearing the same item). *(Icy Veins, Jul 30, 2026 — single-source commentary, `speculation`.)* Note Leoric's Crown is also one of the two legacy Uniques with **no** Unique Charm variant (Blizzard launch blog), a separate system.
- The task requires no skill-point or paragon investment to equip them, and no source lists a level gate — Icy Veins says collection begins "incredibly early" in the season.

## Amalgamation — the upgrade path

- **Recipe:** combine Soul Splinters of the **same type and the same rarity** in the Horadric Cube; the result is one splinter of that type at the **next rarity up**. (Blizzard season blog, Sept 15; repeated verbatim by Maxroll's season guide.)
- **Drops:** splinters drop as **Magic, Rare, Legendary or Unique** (Blizzard launch blog). Upgrade target rarity = whatever the next step is from what you hold.
- **Rarity-count conflict, unresolved:** the **3.2.0 PTR blog** lists **five** rarities — "Common, Magic, Rare, Legendary, and Unique" — while the **launch season blog** lists **four** ("drop as Magic, Rare, Legendary, or Unique"). Both texts are quoted directly; nothing reconciles them. Two observations, neither of them official: every published effect ladder (above) has exactly **four** steps, and no source has ever printed a Common-tier splinter effect — which is consistent with the launch blog's four tiers. **Treat four as the working assumption, verify in game.**
- **Scope:** the Amalgamation rule as published covers upgrades *within* a splinter type. It does **not** say you can convert one splinter type into another (no Anguish → Sin recipe is described anywhere).

### What the upgrade genuinely costs — not published

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| How many splinters are consumed per step? | **Not published.** Blizzard's notes give no input count. Maxroll's Amalgamation table does not list Soul Splinters at all. |
| Gold cost per step? | **Not published.** No source states a gold figure. |
| Crafting-material cost per step? | **Not published.** No source names the material(s). |
| Which content drops which splinter? | **Not published.** Blizzard says only that they drop. |

- The general Horadric Cube Amalgamation pattern elsewhere is **5x of an item → 1 upgraded item** (5x Nightmare Sigils → 1 Escalation Sigil; 5x Magic Runes → 1 Rare Rune; 5x Grand Gem → 1 Horadric Gem — Maxroll, Horadric Cube, Aug 26, 2026). **That is a pattern, not a splinter cost.** Restating it as "5 splinters per step" would be extrapolation, and no confirmed splinter figure exists at day 2. Do not put a number in a build plan.
- One low-trust page (timesaver.gg — boosting/RMT-adjacent, PTR-derived, uncorroborated) claims upgrades run five tiers with gold + materials per step and cites the Unique Lies ladder's 40/60/80/100% — it agrees with the *effect* ladder but is the only place asserting a five-tier cost structure, and it contradicts the launch blog's four drop tiers on the same page. Tagged `speculation`; not promoted here.
- Practical read: with the input count unknown, **do not plan around target-farming a Unique splinter.** At day 2 the actionable loop is to keep duplicates of the same type+rarity and amalgamate opportunistically, banking the result rather than budgeting for it.

## Build-relevant reads (leads, ranked by how well they survive scrutiny)

| Splinter | Fits | Why / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Hellfire | Guaranteed-crit or crit-independent big-hit builds | −100% crit chance is near-free where crit is already guaranteed. A low-trust lead (IGGM, day 2) pairs it with **Ring of Red Furor** (Barbarian big-hit archetypes), plus Crown of Lucion or Ring of Starless Skies to raise resource spend and trigger Red Furor more often. *(single low-trust source, PTR-based — lead only.)* |
| Anguish | Builds with resource-to-spare or spend-scaling mechanics | +30% resource cost hurts sustained spenders; crit chance **and** attack speed in one socket is the strongest offensive line in the set. |
| Sin | Low-max-resource builds, or anything resource-agnostic | +40%[x] max health at the top tier is the largest single defensive number here; the −30% resource pool is the cost. |
| Lies | Gold/MF and XP farming characters, alt leveling | 100% gold + material drop rate with +25% XP is a pure farming socket — but **permanently Weakened and Vulnerable** means you must not fight with it. Swap it out for bossing. |
| Pain | Tanks that already give up mobility | 40% damage reduction is huge; −30% movement speed makes it unusable for speed farms or Lilith-style positioning. |
| the Mother | Melee crowd-clearers who can count | Unhindered + free Vulnerable application is strong uptime; the stun at 100–250 touches lands exactly in density content, so it is a farming liability and a boss non-factor (few touches). |
| the Black Soulstone | Bossers with long kill windows | Damage ramps while the soul window is up, but **stacks never refresh** — the same mechanic that raises damage raises incoming damage, uncapped. |
| Damnation | Players who want difficulty, not power | Monster Power +1 per elite pack cleared is a **deliberate** push lever (Pit, Waking Nightmares), not a stat gem. Worst general-purpose pick; best targeted pick. |

## Open questions

- **Does the Black Soulstone splinter count souls from Warlock demon-consumption?** Unresolved and load-bearing — Rob2628 explicitly conditions his "Warlock is #1" verdict on this interaction (Sep 15). If demon-consumption kills feed the soul stack, the Black Soulstone becomes a Warlock signature. **Re-test after week 1.** *(See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Guide - Season 15 Tier List & Starters|Season 15 Tier List & Starters]] and [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Build & Character/Reference - Season 15 Class Changes 3.2.1|Season 15 Class Changes 3.2.1]].)*
- **Four or five rarities?** Verify in game against the Cube UI.
- **Amalgamation input count and currency** — unstated by every authoritative source; log the first in-game confirmation.
- **Which content drops splinters** — Blizzard has said nothing; if a per-content table appears, it becomes the farming guide for this item class.
- Splinters are a **loot-economy** item as much as a build item: they displace normal jewelry gems and add another sink for Cube materials. Cost context for the Cube generally: [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Guide - Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy|Season 15 Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy]], and for the system they sit inside, [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Reference - Season 15 New Systems|Season 15 New Systems]].
