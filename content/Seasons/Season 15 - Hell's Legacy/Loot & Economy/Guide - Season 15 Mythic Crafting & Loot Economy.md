> [!d4-s15] Mythic Crafting & Loot Economy
> Three Mythic routes · Cube "Upgrade Direct" = **1:1 transfer**, affixes/Tempering/Masterworking kept, carries a `Crafted` tag (one equipped max) · its cost is **published nowhere** · Whispers Gold Caches **≈3x (35M → ≈100M @ T12)** · SSF **still disabled**

## The three Mythic routes in 3.2.1

| Route | Where | What you supply | What comes out | Crafted tag | Cost published? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Upgrade Direct Item to Mythic** | Horadric Cube | one Unique (cannot be Unmodifiable) | **that same item**, now Mythic — affixes, Tempering and Masterworking retained | **Yes** | **No** |
| **Rune Crafting > Mythic Uniques** | Jeweler | runes + materials | random class-usable Mythic from a slot you pick | No | No |
| **Forge > Mythic Cache** (two caches: class-appropriate, and **Iconic-only**) | Blacksmith | cache cost; Iconic cache costs the same as the normal one | random Mythic / random **Iconic** Mythic | No | No |

(Blizzard News, Sept 12 2026; Icy Veins, Sept 13 2026)

### Upgrade Direct: 1:1, and the S14 random roll is gone

- The S14 recipe rolled a **random Mythic of the same item slot** and did **not** retain Greater Affixes, affixes, or even the same item. S15 is a **total 1:1 transfer** (Blizzard, 3.2.1 notes; Icy Veins, Sept 13).
- **Blocked on Unmodifiable items** — Transfiguring a Mythic candidate permanently forecloses this route. Decide whether an item is a Cube candidate *before* you Transfigure it.
- The `Crafted` tag is the price of determinism: **only one Crafted-tag item can be equipped**, and Jeweler/Cache Mythics do **not** get the tag (Blizzard, Sept 12; Icy Veins, Jul 30 PTR dev note, wording unchanged at launch).
- **Cost: unpublished.** Blizzard calls Upgrade Direct "more expensive" than the Blacksmith/Jeweler routes but publishes **no input count and no material cost**. The full notes text contains no quantified Mythic-craft recipe (searched for "Resplendent Spark", "Pandemonium", "cost"). *(verified absence, Blizzard 3.2.1 notes)*

### What the low-trust sources claim — do not build on these

| Source | Claimed Upgrade Direct cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| mmoexp.com | 5 Resplendent Sparks per item | `low-trust` (RMT site), snippet only, page never fetched, corroborated by nobody |
| timesaver.gg | Pandemonium Fragments **plus** the base Unique | `low-trust` (boosting site), PTR-derived, uncorroborated |

The two low-trust figures cannot be reconciled with Blizzard's silence **or with each other**. The per-attempt Fragment count is **unresolved across every source** — do not quote 4x, 5x or "5 Sparks" as an S15 fact.

## Iconic Mythics — chasing a *named* one is still a roll

- **Iconic Mythics remain Mythic-quality only.** The 3.2.0 PTR's **Unique-quality** "Iconic Mythic" items were scrapped after feedback that they cheapened the chase (Blizzard dev note, Sept 12; Icy Veins, Jul 30).
- A **second Blacksmith Mythic Cache** was added that rolls **only Iconic Mythics**, at the **same cost** as the normal cache — because the PTR build had removed the only streamlined way to target Iconics (Blizzard, Sept 12).
- Net effect: the deterministic route **Mythic-ises whatever base Unique you feed it** — it does **not** pin a specific Iconic. The Iconic cache is still a random roll inside the Iconic pool, so a named Iconic chase stays stochastic. *(community-consensus — a reading of the two confirmed statements above, not a Blizzard quote)*
- Practical split: Cube = "make *this* good Unique into a Mythic"; Jeweler = "get *any* Mythic"; Iconic cache = "get *a* named Iconic".

## 3.2.1 drop-rate changes

| Source / item | Change | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Legendary items, Torment I–V** | drop rates **increased**; **Torment VI+ unchanged**. Dev note: reduce reliance on Cube-upgrading random Legendaries to fill the Codex of Power | confirmed |
| **All Uniques** | now drop from **level 1** and in **any Difficulty** | confirmed |
| **Greater Lair Bosses** (Mythics) | slightly increased | confirmed |
| **Initiate Lair Bosses** | Mythic rates **raised to match** Greater Lair Bosses | confirmed |
| **Belial** | slightly increased Mythic rate | confirmed |
| **Random sources (everywhere else)** | increased Mythic drop chance | confirmed |
| **Echo of Mephisto** | overall loot quality up, including a **guaranteed Mythic** | confirmed |
| **Boss Mythic pools** | Lair-Boss and Belial's Hoard Mythics are now **restricted to that boss's own Unique pool** (generic boss loot still carries a small chance outside it) | confirmed |
| **Horadric Cube dust weighting** | Pure Primordial Dust drop weighting **reduced** overall (explicitly because Legendary rates rose); Coarse becomes less common with difficulty; Refined / Volatile / Pure / Enhanced / Attuned become **more** common with difficulty | confirmed |
| **Elites, Torment X+** | significantly more **Gem Fragments**, and significantly higher chance of a **random Royal Gem** | confirmed |

(All Blizzard News, Sept 12 2026; mirrored by Maxroll's 3.2.1 patch-notes page)

Two of these are strategy changes, not just buffs:

- **The boss drop-pool restriction is a target-farming cost increase.** You can no longer pull an off-pool Mythic off a boss you happen to be farming — random sources are now the only outlet for the off-pool table.
- **Dust weighting moved toward high difficulties.** Coarse Primordial Dust drops get scarcer as Torment rises while the higher tiers get commoner, so low-Torment Cube spam gets relatively worse.

## Gold and material economy

### The one quantified S15 gold lever

- **Whispers Gold Caches up ~3x: ≈35 million → ≈100 million Gold at Torment XII** (Blizzard, Sept 12; Maxroll mirror). This is **the only quantified S15 gold figure in an authoritative source**.
- **No gold-per-hour or material-per-hour figure exists from any credible source at day 2.** Maxroll has **no Gold Farming Guide live** — both guide URLs 404 and the S15 Compendium shows a "Gold Farming Guide" image asset with **no link**. Recorded as unavailable, not estimated. *(verified absence)*
- Maxroll's Optimal Farming Guide (last updated **Jul 3, 2026 — pre-S15**) still gives **directional** locations: Boss Lair Keys → Helltides with the "Writhe and Rot" War Plan; Obducite → Horadric Strongrooms; general crafting materials → Greater Material Cache from the Tree of Whispers (Legion events / NMD materials affix / Boss Lair); Cube materials → Nightmare Dungeons with Nemesis + Fearless Conviction War Plans; Gem Fragments → Escalating NMD with Gem Reserve → NMD with Gem Reserve → Seer's Reach spam → Infernal Hordes. *(community-consensus, pre-S15)*
- **Seer's Reach is nerfed relative to that guide**: 3.2.1 ships "Fixed an issue where Royal Gems could repeatedly drop from Seer's Reach dungeon completions." The guide's #3 gem source is now degraded.
- **S14's overlapping-Pandemonium-Rupture gold meta is unverified in S15.** "Pandemonium Rupture" appears nowhere in the 3.2.1 notes and no S15 source corroborates it — neither confirmed still working nor confirmed superseded. *(speculation — negative result)*

| Cache type | 3.2.1 change |

## Sell versus salvage

- **No sell/salvage rule change in 3.2.1** — the patch notes contain **zero** sell-value or salvage-policy entries. *(verified absence, Blizzard 3.2.1 notes + Maxroll mirror)* S15 does not move the rule; it moves the inputs.
- The standing answer remains **salvage**: it yields the crafting materials for Temper/Masterworking, returns socketed Gems to your inventory, and unlocks the item's Transmog. Maxroll also warns against spending materials on the Blacksmith's **random forge** (Maxroll, salvage guide, day 2).
- **S15 tilts further toward salvage:** Salvage Caches buffed **≈2–3x** with the bigger Forgotten Soul bump at high Torment, and the **`Urn of Reclamation`** season blessing boosts the chance of rare materials from salvage (Blizzard, Sept 12).
- *(Inference, not a sourced rule)* Raw gold is now the cheapest it has been — the ~3x cache payout cuts against selling gear for gold at all, since the materials are the constrained resource.

## Gems, fragments and crafting costs

### Cost cuts (Forgotten Souls and Sigil Powder)

| Action | Before → After |
|---|---|
| Craft a **Royal Gem** (Jeweler) | 30 → **10** Forgotten Souls |
| Craft a **Grand Gem** (Jeweler) | 300 → **50** Forgotten Souls |
| Enchant an **Ancestral Item** (Occultist) | 25 → **10** Forgotten Souls |
| Random **Magic Tribute** (Occultist) | 200 → **100** Sigil Powder |
| **Infernal Horde Compass** (Occultist) | 666 → **500** Sigil Powder |

### Sigil Powder salvage returns — flattened 25s become rarity-scaled

| Salvaged item | Before → After |
|---|---|
| Nightmare Dungeon Sigil | 25 → 20 |
| Magic Tribute | 25 → 20 |
| Escalation Sigil | 25 → **100** |
| Infernal Horde Compass | 25 → **100** |
| Rare Tribute | 25 → **100** |
| Legendary Tribute | 25 → **500** |
| **Mythic Tribute** | 25 → **2,500** |

(Blizzard News, Sept 12 2026; Icy Veins, Sept 13 2026)

The shape matters more than any single number: everything used to salvage for a flat 25 Powders; now common sigils/tributes return slightly **less** (20) and premium ones return **4x–100x** more. Low-value sigils are no longer a powder tap, and a spare Mythic Tribute is now a powder *cache* — don't vendor or trash the high-end tributes.

### Supply side

- **Gem Fragment supply is up:** Elite gem-fragment drops and Elite Royal-Gem chance both significantly increased in **Torment X+**; Gem Cache fragments **≈2x**; Gem Caches give **1 Royal or Grand Gem** instead of a Flawless Gem (Blizzard, Sept 12).
- **Grand → Horadric progression runs only through the Cube's Amalgamation recipe:** 5x same Grand Gem → 1 Horadric Gem of the same type; 5x same Horadric Gem → 1 **Flawless** Horadric Gem of the same type (Maxroll, Horadric Cube, Aug 26).
- **Soul Splinter upgrade cost is unpublished.** No per-step input count, gold or material figure appears in the 3.2.1 notes, and Maxroll's Amalgamation table does not list Soul Splinters — a "5x splinters" figure would be **extrapolation, not fact**. The general Amalgamation pattern is 5x → 1. Splinter rarities and effects live in [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Loot & Economy/Reference - Season 15 Soul Splinters|Season 15 Soul Splinters]]. *(verified absence)*
- `low-trust` claim, not promoted: timesaver.gg describes **five** Soul Splinter rarity tiers (Common → Unique) at gold + materials per step, with a Unique Splinter of Lies granting +100% Gold Drop Rate and +25% Bonus XP and scaling 40/60/80/100%. That contradicts Blizzard's own **four** drop tiers (Magic/Rare/Legendary/Unique) and is PTR-derived. *(speculation)*

## Trading and SSF

- **No trading changes in 3.2.1.** The patch notes contain **no trade-policy entries at all** — the only "Trade" hits are the **Tricks of the Trade** Rogue Paragon node and a related bug fix. *(verified absence)* Tradeability rules in S15 are the same as the pre-season baseline.
- **SSF is still disabled.** **Hotfix 1 (Sept 15, 2026)** resolved login blocking and reopened the Seasonal Realm, but Solo Self Found "will remain unavailable while the team continues its investigation"; Maxroll still lists **"SSF Mode Temporarily Disabled"** on Sept 16 (Icy Veins, Sept 15, quoting Blizzard's Hotfix 1 forum post; Maxroll news index, Sept 16 — headline/date only, body not fetched).
- **Consequence:** there is **no SSF-specific economy or price data**, and **no S15 trade-rate data** (gold-per-item, Rune or Mythic prices) from any source at day 2. All S15 economy coverage comes from trade/party-enabled realms. *(verified absence)*
- For SSF planning, run off the drop-rate and cache figures in this note rather than off market prices — there is no market to read yet.

## Runewords shadow-dropped at launch (biggest economy change not in the notes)

- **New "classic" Runewords arrived at S15 launch without appearing in the patch notes**: a normal-quality base item plus the required Runes, transmuted in the **Horadric Cube**, produces a specific Unique. Separate from the existing D4 rune system (Icy Veins, Sept 15; Maxroll, Sept 16).
- Icy Veins lists 19 recipes, including **Enigma** (Jah Ith Ber), **Infinity** (Ber Mal Ber Ist), **Grief** (Eth Tir Lo Mal Ral), **Call to Arms** (Amn Ral Mal Ist Ohm), **Spirit** (Tal Thul Ort Amn), **Phoenix** (Vex Vex Lo Jah), **Insight** (Ral Tir Tal Sol), **Lore** (Ort Sol) and **Stealth** (Tal Eth).
- **New Runes added:** Ritual Runes **Amn, Eth, Mal, Ort, Tal**; Invocation Runes **Ber, Ist** (Maxroll names the full new set: Amn, Ber, Eth, Ist, Ith, Lo, Mal, Ort, Ral, Sol, Tal, Tir).
- Many Runewords work in **multiple base types** with differing stats (Spirit Sword vs Spirit Shield).
- **Economy implication:** deterministic, Rune-priced routes to specific Uniques — including top-tier D2 items — are likely the **single biggest S15 economy shake-up**. **No Rune or base-item price data exists at day 2.** *(community-consensus, day 2)* See [[Seasons/Season 15 - Hell's Legacy/Endgame Systems/Reference - Season 15 New Systems|Season 15 New Systems]].

## Season-level crafting rewards

- **Season Ranks** (9 ranks, 125 objectives): up to **14 Skill Points**, **42 Paragon** points, up to **10 Resplendent Sparks** (plus those in Mythic Caches), **5 Mythic Unique Caches**, plus crafting/masterworking materials, currency, Lair Boss Keys, and caches containing Runes, Sigils, Talisman Charms and Seals (Blizzard, Sept 12).
- **Economy-relevant Season Blessings:** `Urn of Reclamation` (rarest materials from salvage), `Urn of Masterworking` (extra Obducite), `Urn of Curiosities` (second item chance from the Purveyor), `Urn of Ancestral Whispers` (Ancestral Cache chance on Whisper turn-ins).
- **≈15% of Seasonal Objectives require the Lord of Hatred expansion**, including objectives needed to complete Rank VIII and earn all Season Rank rewards.

## Open questions and stale-source traps

- **Maxroll's Horadric Cube page is stale for S15.** Last updated **Aug 26, 2026**, it still describes the pre-S15 recipe: *"Transmutes a Unique equipment item with at least 850 Item Power into a random Mythic of the same item slot… does not retain Greater Affixes, Affixes, nor is it guaranteed to be the same item. Required: 1x Unique Item, 5x Pandemonium Fragment."* That **directly contradicts** Blizzard's S15 "total 1:1 transfer". **Trust Blizzard.** The page's changelog stops at Aug 26 — it has not been touched for S15.
- **The Pandemonium Fragment per-attempt count is unresolved.** Maxroll says **5x** (old recipe); timesaver.gg says the S14 baseline was **4x** (after a 3.1.1 reduction) and that the S15 count was never published; nexttier.pro's S14 guide also says 4x, reduced from 5. **No official S15 figure exists.**
- **No g/hr, material/hr or trade-rate figures exist from any credible source at day 2.** Day-2 economy numbers are volatile by nature; re-run these in ~2 weeks.
- **Wowhead contributed nothing** to this cluster — its D4 news index is dominated by WoW/BlizzCon items and carried no S15 economy, drop-rate or crafting article (item database not queried within budget). **SearXNG** returned irrelevant domains for 6+ economy-targeted queries; all substantive data came from direct fetches of Blizzard, Maxroll and Icy Veins.
- **Icy Veins' crafting article and Maxroll's launch article quote the same Blizzard source** — they corroborate wording, not independent testing. Where "confirmed" rests on those two, the underlying authority is Blizzard.
