# Advanced — Weapon Transfigure Gambling

> Source: German creator advanced crafting guide (May 22, 2026)

**This method is not required for any content.** You can clear Torment 12 and kill Uber Mephisto without ever doing this. It is a pure endgame chase for players chasing ladder rankings or looking for any incremental upgrade.

---

## The Core Concept

Normal crafting wisdom says: **Transfigure is always the last step** — temper, masterwork, socket, imprint, then finally Transfigure.

This method inverts that: **Transfigure first, invest later.**

The logic is simple:
- Transfiguring a raw, uninvested weapon costs 1 Volatile Primordial Dust — **nothing else**
- If it lands a good outcome (Gem Strength, +Ultimate Skills) **and stays modifiable**, you can then temper, masterwork, enchant, and socket it properly
- If it becomes unmodifiable or gets a bad outcome, you've lost nothing — you were going to salvage that weapon anyway

### Why This Works

Most GA weapons you find will never be your BiS — they have wrong affixes, wrong weapon type, or your build just doesn't need them. Instead of insta-salvaging, turn every GA weapon into a **free lottery ticket** before scrapping it.

### The Procedure

1. Pick up every GA weapon, regardless of affixes
2. Do NOT temper, masterwork, enchant, or socket
3. Go to the Horadric Cube → **Transfigure Item**
4. Check the outcome:
   - **Stayed modifiable + good roll (Gem Strength, Ultimate Skills, Item Quality)** → keep, build out properly
   - **Stayed modifiable + bad roll** → can Transfigure again for a chance at a second roll
   - **Became unmodifiable** → salvage, move on
5. Repeat with every GA weapon you find

## Target Outcomes

### S-Tier — Gem Strength ⭐

Multiplies the effectiveness of the gem socketed in your weapon. This is the jackpot.

| Gem | Base Effect | With Gem Strength | With 333% GS (top ladder) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flawless Horadric (weapon) | 32% multiplicative | ~72% | 139% |
| Horadric (weapon) | 28% multiplicative | ~62% | ~120% |

**How the #1 barbarian got 333% gem strength:**
1. Transfigured a weapon → got Gem Strength + item stayed modifiable
2. Transfigured AGAIN (multi-Transfiguration) → stacked another Gem Strength roll
3. Masterworked the item → **crit landed on Gem Strength**, pushing it to 333%
4. Slotted Horadric jewels → 139% multiplicative damage from gems alone

### A-Tier — +Ultimate Skills

- Up to **+6 ranks to Ultimate Skills** on a weapon
- Massive for builds where the ultimate is the main damage skill (Call of the Ancients, Army of the Dead, etc.)
- Equivalent to multiple skill rank affixes without occupying a slot

### B-Tier — Item Quality / Extra GA

- Item Quality increases the base stats of the weapon
- Extra GA adds an implicit stat line
- Less impactful than Gem Strength or Ultimate Skills, but still a meaningful upgrade

## The Entropic Prism Trap

**Never use Entropic Tuning Prisms for weapon Transfiguration.**

Here's why:

| | No Prism (Raw) | With Entropic Prism |
|---|---|---|
| Unmodifiable chance | ~95% | **100% guaranteed** |
| Can multi-Transfigure? | Yes (if stays modifiable) | Never |
| Can roll Gem Strength? | Yes | **Blocked** |
| Can roll +Ultimate Skills? | Yes | **Blocked** |
| Can roll Item Quality? | Yes | **Blocked** |
| Worst outcome | "Indestructible" (still modifiable) | Unmodifiable + weak roll |

The Entropic Prism flattens the outcome table and blocks both the best AND the most dangerous results. But the worst natural outcome ("Indestructible") is harmless — you just salvage it. The upside (Gem Strength, Ultimate Skills) is irreplaceable.

**Rule:** Never Entropic on weapons. Ever. Only consider it on an irreplaceable 4GA armor piece you can't afford to lose.

## The Math

Real results from testing:

- **~30 GA weapons** → 1 that stays modifiable
- **~200+ weapons** → 1 Gem Strength or Ultimate Skills outcome
- Cost per attempt: **1 Volatile Primordial Dust** (zero gold, zero other materials)

You accumulate GA weapons naturally through normal play (Tree of Whispers, Helltide, Undercity, bosses, War Plans). The only cost is a brief detour to the Cube between runs.

### What It Takes

If you play 2-3 hours a day:
- You'll find ~20-30 GA weapons
- Transfigure each one (~2 minutes total per session)
- In a week you'll see 3-5 modifiable results
- In a month you'll likely land one S-tier outcome

## Mythic Gambling

Works the same way with duplicate mythics:
- Take a mythic you don't need (second Grandfather, extra Tyrael's Might, etc.)
- Transfigure it **before** salvaging to Resplendent Sparks
- If it rolls Gem Strength or a build-defining stat, it might outperform what you're currently wearing
- If it bricks → salvage for the spark as usual

With Mythic Tributes of Armaments being farmable via War Plans (see War Plans guide), you'll accumulate enough mythics to make this worthwhile.

## Advanced: Double-Transfigure

If a weapon stays modifiable after the first Transfigure:
1. Don't salvage — **Transfigure again**
2. This adds a second random modifier on top
3. Now you have **two** Transfiguration outcomes on one item

**Only pursue this if:**
- You have multiple modifiable weapons lined up
- The first roll was good but not amazing (e.g. Item Quality on a Gem Strength chase)
- You're prepared to lose the weapon if it bricks on the second roll

The #1 barbarian's 333% gem strength came from exactly this — two stacked Gem Strength rolls.

---

## Building Out a Hit

When you get a modifiable weapon with a good outcome:

1. Temper (Blacksmith)
2. Masterwork (Blacksmith) — aim for crit on Gem Strength if you rolled it
3. Enchant (Occultist) — reroll the dud affix
4. Imprint your aspect
5. Socket
6. **Transfigure a third time** if the item still allows it

From that point, you're done. Use the Occultist Lock technique (see crafting guide) to protect key affixes during the process.
