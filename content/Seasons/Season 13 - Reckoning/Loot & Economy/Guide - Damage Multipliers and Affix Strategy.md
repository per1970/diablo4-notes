# Guide — Damage Multipliers and Affix Strategy

> Source: community video (German creator, May 2026) + Maxroll damage guide + Wowhead Horadric Cube guide cross-reference

If you copied a build guide exactly and you're stuck doing billions instead of trillions, the culprit is almost always your **affix selection**. Missing damage multiplier affixes on rings, gloves, and amulet — especially skill ranks on charms — is the gap between billions and trillions.

---

## Damage Buckets — Additive vs Multiplicative

Diablo 4 uses a **bucket system** for damage modifiers. The critical insight: **stacking the same bucket type is additive, mixing different types is multiplicative.**

### The Box Analogy

Imagine your total damage as the volume of a box:

- **Length** = Critical Strike Damage
- **Width** = Vulnerable Damage
- **Height** = All Damage (or elemental damage, or skill ranks)

If you only increase length — 20 + 20 + 20 — you get a long thin line. But if you increase each dimension: 20 × 20 × 20, the volume grows exponentially.

### What This Means for Your Gear

A ring with:
- Critical Strike Damage × 2 (bad — adds together)
- Lightning Damage × 2 (bad — adds together)

...is worse than a ring with:
- Critical Strike Damage × 1
- Vulnerable Damage × 1
- All Damage × 1

...even if the numbers are smaller. The multipliers multiply each other.

**Rule of thumb:** Every piece should have **at least 2 different** damage multiplier types. 3 is ideal. A ring with only one offensive multiplier (plus attack speed, cooldown, etc.) is leaving damage on the table.

### Example From the Stat Sheet

A character showing **3,000% critical strike damage** can drop hundreds of percent just by removing gloves with a single weak multiplier. The "3,000%" displayed already includes the multiplicative cross-bucket interaction — losing one bucket collapses the whole stack.

## Skill Ranks — The Biggest Scaler

Skill ranks function as an **independent damage multiplier**, equivalent to an All Damage affix or better.

### Benchmarks

- **+3 skill ranks** on a 318% multiplier skill (e.g. Boulder) → drops to 297% when removed = **21% loss from 3 ranks**
- **+3 skill ranks** on a 594% multiplier skill (e.g. Call of the Ancients) → drops to 554% = **40% bonus damage**
- **+18 total skill ranks** from 6 charms at +3 each represents a **massive** multiplicative leap — enough to push millions → billions or billions → trillions

### Where to Get Skill Ranks

| Slot | Source | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Gloves | Core skill ranks via **Adapt Tuning Prism** | +3 |
| Amulet | Broader pool: core, basic, zealot, aura, juggernaut, **all skills** | +2 (core) / +3 (specific tag) |
| Charms (×6) | Set Charm skill ranks | +3 per charm |

### Exceptions

Not every build can scale via charm skill ranks. Example: **Army of the Dead Necromancer** — the ultimate skill is tagged as "Ultimate" and "Summon," but lacks a "Minion" tag. No charm provides +Ultimate skills. The +2 Minion skill ranks on charms help minions but not the main nuke. Blizzard has not yet fixed this gap.

## Ring Crafting — Step by Step

Goal: a ring with **2-3 different damage multipliers + attack speed + cooldown reduction.**

### Step 1: Remove Unwanted Stats

Start with a ring that has at least one desirable GA (e.g. Greater Affix Attack Speed). Strip unwanted affixes using **Tuning Prisms** in the Horadric Cube:

| Unwanted Affix Type | Use This Prism |
|---|---|
| Core stat (Str/Dex/Int/Will) | **Adapt** (removes Core Stat / Skill Rank affixes) |
| Maximum Life / Armor / Resist | **Protector** (removes Defensive affixes) |
| Resource generation / resource cost | **Resource** (removes Resource affixes) |
| Any offensive stat | **Aggressive** (removes Offensive affixes) |

### Step 2: Add Aggressive Affixes

After stripping down to 1-2 affixes, add **Aggressive Tuning Prisms** to add offensive affixes. Each add is random, but the pool is limited to offensive stats — you'll see multipliers, crit chance, attack speed, etc.

### Step 3: The Occultist Lock Technique (Key Advanced Trick)

After adding a desirable greater affix (e.g. Physical Damage Multiplier), you can **lock it** so it can never be removed or rerolled:

1. Take the item to the **Occultist**
2. Select **Enchant** on the desired affix (e.g. Physical Damage Multiplier)
3. The Occultist shows reroll options — **choose "No Change"**
4. This **locks the affix** permanently — it becomes untargetable by any Cube recipe

Now go back to the Cube. The locked affix is immune to all future Focused Rerolls. You can **freely reroll other slots** without ever touching the protected one. Rerolling in the Cube costs only Tuning Prisms — no gold.

**Downside:** The Occultist can no longer reroll that affix either. The lock is permanent. Only use this technique after you're satisfied with the roll, or as a way to protect a hard-won GA while you finish other slots.

### Step 4: Refine Remaining Slo

With the locked affix safe, add Affixes with Aggressive prisms and use **Focused Reroll** (Refined Primordial Dust + Tuning Prism) to fish for multiplier types until you hit your target combo.

### Step 5: Enchant the Leftover Dud

Once you have 3-4 affixes and one is a dud (e.g. Holy Damage, Strength you don't want), go back to the **Occultist** and **Enchant** that specific affix into something useful — Cooldown Reduction, a different multiplier, or Crit Chance.

**Why step 5 is last:** Enchanting at the Occultist costs gold and gets expensive with each reroll. Only do it after all Cube work is done so you're enchanting the final slot.

**Final ring example:** Critical Strike Damage + Vulnerable Damage + Attack Speed + Cooldown Reduction (or All Damage / Physical Damage). Two multipliers + utility stats.

## Glove Crafting

### The Adapt Prism Trick

Gloves are hardcoded to roll **Core Skill Ranks** from the **Adapt Tuning Prism**. This is the most repeatable source of bonus skill ranks in the game:

1. Start with gloves that have a GA on Attack Speed, Crit Chance, or a damage multiplier
2. Use **Adapt Tuning Prism** to remove Intelligence or Core Stats
3. Add the Adapt prism again — it can roll into **+Core Skills** on the new affix
4. Repeat step 2-3 until you land +3 Core Skills
5. Use the Focused Reroll method (or Occultist lock if you also have a good multiplier)

Since the Adapt prism has a limited pool for gloves (Core Skills or core stat), you can force-roll skill ranks reliably. 20-40 prisms is typical.

### Ideal Glove Rolls

- Attack Speed (GA preferred)
- Crit Chance
- Vulnerable Damage
- **+Core Skills** (or Crit Damage if you have skill ranks elsewhere)
- Or: All Damage + Vulnerable + Crit Damage + Attack Speed (if skill ranks come from charms)

## Amulet Crafting

### Wider Pool Than Gloves

Amulets can roll into more skill tag types than gloves:

Basic, Core, Zealot, Aura, Justice, Valor, Disciple, Juggernaut, or **All Skills**

### "All Skills" is the Jackpot

- **All Skills** on an amulet boosts every active skill and aura simultaneously — your main skill, auras, ultimate, utility
- Rolls at **+2** instead of +3 that a specific tag would get — but the efficiency gain from hitting every skill category usually outweighs the -1 rank penalty
- Adapt Tuning Prism can force-roll toward skill ranks, but the wider pool means more RNG cycles

### Amulet Strategy

1. Get a base with a useful GA (damage multiplier, cooldown, movement speed)
2. Strip unwanted stats with Tuning Prisms
3. Add aggressive affixes and Focused Reroll until you land **All Skills**, your specific tag, or a triple-multiplier combo
4. Occultist lock the skill rank affix if it's a keeper
5. Enchant the last slot

Expect 20-40 Adapt prisms if fishing for All Skills. Worth it — you do this once per build.

## Farming Infused Horadric Resin

Rerolling set charms for skill ranks consumes Infused Horadric Resin. This is the main bottleneck.

| Source | Resin Per Unit |
|---|---|
| Salvage: Magic Charm | ~5 |
| Salvage: Rare Charm | ~10 |
| Salvage: Set Charm | ~25 |
| Salvage: Magic Seal | ~8 |
| Salvage: Rare Seal | ~20 |
| Salvage: Legendary Seal | ~60 |

### Tips

- **Pick up Rare charms and seals.** Don't filter them out. The 10/20 resin per pop adds up fast.
- **Skip Magic quality** — not worth the inventory space.
- **Salvage Legendary seals** — 60 resin is a meaningful chunk, and selling them for gold is rarely worth more than resin.
- **Reroll Set Charms** at the Horadric Cube: 1 Set Charm + 25 Raw Primordial Dust + 50 Resin → new charm in the same set. Hold down **F** (or **Y** on controller) to skip the transmute animation — you can chain-reroll without re-entering the menu.

## Mercenary — Rahier

Rahier works best for **every build** in Season 13.

### Skill Tree Path

- **Right side:** Armor or Resistance (90% of cases take Resistance)
- **Bastion** — allies affected deal **25% more damage** (huge, always take this)
- **Inspiration** — enemies affected by Rahier's ground slam take **15% increased damage**

### Alternatives

| Skill | Effect | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Mocking Lure | Increased damage vs. taunted enemies | Tanky melee builds |
| Iron Wolf's Call | Next 4 Core skills within 10s deal 25% more damage at no resource cost | Core skill builds |

Only switch from Rahier/Bastion if your build has a specific synergy with another mercenary's call.

## Quick Reference Card

### Damage Multiplier Priority

| Affix | Notes |
|---|---|
| All Damage | Universal, works with everything |
| Vulnerable Damage | Requires vulnerable application |
| Critical Strike Damage | Requires crit chance |
| Skill Ranks | Independent multiplier, check your tags |
| Physical / Elemental Damage | Build-dependent |

### Crafting Recipe Cheat Sheet

| Goal | Recipe | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Remove unwanted affix | Remove Affix + Tuning Prism | Refined Dust + Raw Dust |
| Add offensive affix | Add Affix + Aggressive Prism | Coarse Dust + Raw Dust |
| Reroll a specific affix | Focused Reroll + Tuning Prism | Refined Dust + Raw Dust |
| Lock a GA permanently | Occultist Enchant → "No Change" | Gold only |
| Reroll leftover dud | Occultist Enchant | Gold (expensive per roll) |

### Masterwork Before Tempering

Always **masterwork first, then temper.** Masterworking increases item level, which affects tempering outcomes. Tempering a fully masterworked item lets you fish for greater affixes more efficiently.
