# Guide — Corrupted Roots NMD Farming

> Source: German creator NMD farm guide (May 22, 2026) + Reddit discovery cross-reference
> ⚠️ Roots traded for ~900M–1B on Diablo.Trade. Don't buy them — farm them passively.

A Shrine-sigil Nightmare Dungeon combined with Corrupted Roots and the right War Plan nodes creates a **respawning enemy cascade** that can drop hundreds of items, dozens of GAs, thousands of Forgotten Souls, and a Conviction chest stacked to 250+ in a single run.

---

## How It Works

The chain relies on three mechanics stacking together:

1. **Corrupted Roots** (placed at shrine locations) — spawn ambush waves with +1 monster power
2. **Gauntlet** (War Plan node) — enemies killed while shrine-buffed respawn when the buff expires
3. **Nemesis** (War Plan node) — activating a shrine can spawn 7 elite ambushers
4. **Fearless Conviction** (War Plan node) — killing elites/champions builds Conviction stacks, with each stack improving the end-of-dungeon Conviction chest

Each shrine becomes a multi-phase encounter:
- Plant roots → activate shrine → wave 1 (roots ambush + normal enemies)
- Wait for shrine buff to expire → **entire wave respawns** (Gauntlet)
- Press shrine again → kill respawned wave + 7 Nemesis elites
- Wait for buff to expire → **respawning again** for a third cycle

With 3-4 shrines per dungeon, you get 9-12 kill cycles from a single run.

## Step-by-Step Execution

### Prerequisites

- A **Nightmare Dungeon sigil** with any Shrine affix (Lethal Shrines, Artillery Shrines, Blast Wave Shrines — any works)
- **3-5 Corrupted Roots** (see Farming Roots section)
- **War Plan points** invested (see below)

### Step 1 — Scout the Dungeon

Enter the NMD and **clear all enemies but do not use any shrines**. Find every shrine in the dungeon:
- Most dungeons have **3 shrines**; rare cases have 4
- Do not enter the boss room
- Do not finish the dungeon
- Memorize or mark shrine locations

### Step 2 — Plant Roots

Return to the first shrine. Plant **3-4 Corrupted Roots** at the shrine location:
- Place one root
- Teleport out and back in (resets the 30-second cooldown between placements)
- Place the next root
- Repeat

Don't go overboard — 3-4 is enough. Too many roots can make the encounter unmanageable.

### Step 3 — Activate and Kill

1. Activate the shrine
2. Root ambush enemies spawn (with +1 monster power, Head Rotten boss possible)
3. Kill everything
4. **Wait for the shrine buff to fully expire**
5. Gauntlet triggers — **all enemies respawn**
6. Press the shrine again immediately
7. Nemesis spawns 7 extra elites on top of the respawned wave
8. Kill everything again
9. Buff expires again — **third respawn wave**
10. Kill a third time
11. Move to the next shrine and repeat

### Step 4 — Finish the Dungeon

After cycling all shrines (9-12 kill phases), kill the boss and collect:

- **War Chest** (from the War Plan bounty)
- **Conviction Chest** — you should have 200-300 Conviction stacks by now, making this chest extremely dense with loot, mythic potential, seals, and charms
- Normal boss loot

## War Plan Requirements

### Minimum (4 points)

| Node | Points | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Waking Spoils** | 1 | Bonus chest at end of NMD |
| **Gauntlet** | 1 | Shrine-buffed enemies respawn on buff expiry |
| **Nemesis** | 1 | Activating shrine spawns 7 elite ambushers |
| **Fearless Conviction** | 1 | Kill elites for conviction stacks → better end chest |

### Optional Cross-Tree Upgrades

| Tree | Node | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Helltide** → Tainted Shrines → Hated Foe → Nigh Terrors | 3 | Replace Hellborn with **Butchers** in NMDs → Butchers drop Profane Mind Cages |
| **Lair Bosses** → Out of the Cold | 1 | Beast of Ice can appear during NMD boss fights |
| **Lair Bosses** → Dock of Astaroth | 1 | Astaroth's dog can appear during NMD boss fights |

With the Helltide cross-tree, Butchers will spawn repeatedly during the shrine cycles, each dropping a Profane Mind Cage for overworld monster power buffs.

## Farming Corrupted Roots

Corrupted Roots are a **Tree of Whispers drop** — you get them passively from normal gameplay, not from dedicated farming.

### Best Sources

| Source | Drop Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Collection of Chaos** (Whisper Cache) | ~1 in 4 | Best source — pick this over Mysteries |
| **Cache of Materials** (via Tree of Plenty) | Good | 4-choice node improves odds |
| **Whisper Ambush caches** (Grim Retribution tree) | Moderate | Ambushed bosses drop whisper-level caches |
| **Head Rotten bosses** (from roots themselves) | Can chain | Root spawns boss → boss drops whisper cache → may contain more roots |

### Tree of Whispers Nodes That Help

| Node | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Corrupted Roots** | Roots sometimes appear in whisper caches |
| **Roots of Power** | Increases monster power of root enemies → better rewards |
| **Head Rotten Feast** | Head Rotten bosses can appear from roots; drop whisper caches |
| **Grim Retribution** | Whisper ambushes can contain more roots |
| **Colossal Foes** | Ambush bosses have better loot tables |
| **Tree of Plenty** | 4 whisper cache choices instead of 3 |

### The Passive Approach

Don't force-farm roots. Just:
1. Do your normal War Plan activities
2. Pick **Collection of Chaos** from whisper caches
3. Spec the Tree of Whispers nodes above when you have spare War Plan points
4. Stockpile roots over a few sessions
5. When you have 4-5 roots, run a dedicated shrine NMD

## Escalation Sigil Variant

You can also apply this technique to **Nightmare Dungeon Escalation Sigils** (crafted from 5 useless NMD keys at the Horadric Cube).

Escalation sigils are even better because:
- The dungeon scales across multiple floors
- More shrines per run = more cycles
- Super Astaroth (Giga Astaroth) has boosted mythic drop tables
- Bosses respawn with +1 monster power (via Recurring Nightmare node)

**Recommended War Plan nodes for escalation runs:**

| Node | Effect |
|---|---|
| Waking Spoils | Bonus end chest |
| Recurring Nightmare | Bosses gain +1 monster power |
| Boss Respawn | Bosses can revive when slain = double loot |
| Extra Horadric Portals | Additional value rooms (skip if they slow you down) |

## What to Expect

Per well-executed run:

- **200-300 Conviction stacks** → massive Conviction chest
- **Multiple GA items** (often fill an inventory)
- **Thousands of Forgotten Souls** (root ambush drops + packs)
- **Tuning Prisms, charms, seals** from repeated elite kills
- **Potential mythics** from Conviction chest
- **Profane Mind Cages** (if cross-tree Butcher nodes active)

Total time: ~5-8 minutes per run (scouting + 3 shrine cycles + boss kill).

- [ ] Plant 3-4 roots at first shrine (teleport out between each)

- [ ] Move to next shrine, repeat
- [ ] Kill boss, open War Chest + Conviction chest
- [ ] Check for GAs, mythics, Profane Mind Cages
