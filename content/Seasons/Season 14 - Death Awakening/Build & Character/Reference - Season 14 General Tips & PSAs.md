## Setup & UI

### Loot Filter Can Hide Mythics — Check Yours
A blanket "Hide All Uniques" rule also hides **mythic uniques** (purple). Mythics are a subtype of unique. **Periodically turn off your loot filter** to verify nothing purple is being hidden. Solution: create explicit "Show" rules for specific uniques your build needs, rather than blanket Hide Unique rules.

### Enchanter "Equip" Search Filter
At the Enchanter (Occultist), type **"equip"** into the search box to see only aspects you currently have equipped. Hover each result to see which exact item it's on — trivial to spot upgrades without scrolling.

### Infernal Hordes Without a Key
If Infernal Hordes is selected in War Plans and you click **"Teleport to Dungeon"**, you enter **without spending a key**. Alternatively, Party Finder to join someone else's Horde run. Free entry, free loot.

### Party Finder for Content You Can't Access
No sigils/keys for a specific activity? Open Party Finder and join someone else's session at your desired tier. Works for Hordes, Undercity, and boss fights.

## Crafting & Gearing Traps

### ONE Crafted Mythic Limit (Cube + Jeweler + Blacksmith)
You can only equip **one** crafted mythic across your entire build. This applies to ALL three crafting sources: Cube (mythic upgrade), Jeweler (rune crafting), and Blacksmith (iconic mythics). Check the tooltip — if it says "Crafted," it counts. Plan which single crafted mythic matters most.

### Cube Mythic Upgrade is RANDOM
Feeding a god-rolled unique to the cube gives a **random mythic of the same slot type** — NOT the same item. A perfect Tuskhelm does NOT give a mythic Tuskhelm; it gives a random mythic helm. Never feed your best-in-slot unique into the cube.

### DO NOT Salvage All Keys — Destroys Tributes
The Occultist's **"Salvage All Keys"** button destroys Tributes of Armament (mythic tributes), Infernal Horde compasses, and other non-sigil keys. Only Escalating NM sigils are excluded. **Favorite all tributes** before salvaging, or use "Salvage Junk Keys."

### Gem Crafting Cost Halved
Gems now require only **two** material types instead of three. Rubies cost Sapphires + Topaz (no Diamonds). Escalating NM dungeons drop 250k-750k fragments per chest/boss — one boss kill can give nearly an entire Grand Gem's worth.

### 850 IP Gear is Fine on Penitent — Don't Rush Torment
You do NOT need Torment 1 to get 850 IP gear. Stay on **Penitent** until fully geared with 850s and a cohesive build. cliptis86 cleared Pit 100 (Torment 12) wearing 850 gear — affixes, tempers, and masterworking matter more than item power floor.

### Masterwork 850 Gear First — Save Forgotten Souls
850 IP gear only costs **legendary materials** to masterwork. Ancestral (900 IP) costs **Forgotten Souls** — expensive. Veiled Crystals → Baneful Hearts → Forgotten Souls via Alchemist is a new S14 conversion path. Legion events on highest torment drop 200 Forgotten Souls.

## Undocumented Changes

### World Bosses Have Resilience (Again)
World bosses are **significantly tankier** due to re-added Resilience system — not in patch notes. Azmodan is the worst offender. The more players present, the faster they die. On low-pop hours, consider dropping torment for world bosses.

### Pit Protection Bubble Removed
The protective bubble at Pit start is **gone** — you take damage immediately on load-in. Do not AFK at entrance. Especially dangerous on Hardcore: clear the area before upgrading glyphs.

### Mythic Seals Drop Much More Frequently
Mythic Seals — especially **Diamond Mines** — drop at massively increased rates. Players report multiple seal drops on day one, compared to 8-9 total across 600 hours in S13. Great for Diamond Mines set effect builds.

### Drop Charms Can Now Have Greater Affixes
Previously Cube re-roll only. Drop charms now naturally drop with GAs. Don't auto-salvage every charm — check for GA rolls.

### Material Reserve NMDs Are the #1 Farm
NMDs with **Material Reserve** (and escalation affixes) are absurdly good for Tuning Prisms and refined dust. Prioritize Material Reserve sigils. Escalating NM with Branching Pathways lets you pick material rewards per floor.

## Post-70 Priority Checklist

1. **Capstone dungeon #2** (level 60 static) — wait until 70, it's easy then
2. **Collect all glyphs** — drop from Pits, NMDs, Helltides, Whispers
3. **Level glyphs in Pit** — run as high as you can for multiple upgrades per run
4. **Season journey** → 27 paragon points — don't skip
5. **Paragon boards:** Glyph sockets → Legendary nodes → **defensive nodes before offense** — you need to survive
6. **Season reputation → Resplendent Spark** is priority #1 — first guaranteed mythic material
7. **Infernal Hordes:** buy **equipment chest first** (guaranteed 900 IP ancestral), then materials

## Economy & Material Tricks

### Helltides Are the Mythic Key Farm
Helltides are the best source of **Superior Lair Keys** (highest mythic drop chance) and regular Lair Keys. Run War Plans with Writhe and Rote → Profane Mindcages for maximum helltide loot. Greater Helltide Chests (750 cinders) give 3x activity XP.

### Undercity Tributes Reverted to ~30% Mythic Chance
The S13 guaranteed mythic from Tribute of Armament was a bug. Now reverted to ~30%. Still worth running — just don't expect guaranteed drops.

### Obol Vendor Is Best Aspect Source
The obol vendor (Cerrigar) is the only place to **target-farm specific item slots** for aspects. Spam the vendor for slot-specific uniques/aspects once you have obols to burn.

## Mythic 3.0 System Problems ⚠️

Mythic acquisition has become the #1 community pain point in S14. Multiple independent sources confirm significant nerfs and design issues:

### Drop Rate Nerfs
- **Mythic Tributes gutted:** From guaranteed mythic per tribute in S13 to ~30% or worse. Players report 12 chests with zero mythics. wudijo: "giga nerfed."
- **Boss mythic drops nerfed across the board.** Even with farming hundreds of keys, mythic drops are sparse.
- **Rank 1 HC SSF player breakdown (Jul 6):** 46h playtime, Paragon 252, ~100 keys + 100 caches → only **9 Pandemonium Fragments** (not enough for ONE craft). Six Mythic tributes on T12 with Trials and Tributes node → one off-build mythic drop. The season built around Mythic rework makes its centerpiece nearly inaccessible through both crafting AND drops.

### The Resplendent Spark Problem
- Once you craft your **one** mythic (Cube/Jeweler/Blacksmith all share the limit), Resplendent Sparks pile up with no use
- Old system: 2 Sparks = guaranteed mythic. New system: Sparks only used at Jeweler/Blacksmith, which also counts toward the crafted limit
- Salvaging a mythic returns a Spark — but excess Sparks are dead inventory

### Crafted Mythic Confusion
- The "Crafted" tag applies to ALL three sources: Cube, Jeweler, Blacksmith
- **Different mythic pools:** Cube and Jeweler give random class/general mythics. **Blacksmith only** gives Iconic mythics (the old ones: Shako, Grandfather, Starless Skies, etc.)
- You cannot get a Starless Skies from Cube/Jeweler — Blacksmith is the only path
- wudijo confirms: played most of the season without any mythic equipped. Crafted ~30 times at Blacksmith trying for a specific iconic — still unsuccessful

### Practical Takeaway
- Don't hoard Sparks waiting to craft multiple mythics — you can't
- Choose your single crafted mythic wisely: biggest multiplier for your build
- If you need an Iconic mythic (old ones), the Blacksmith is your only crafting path
- Drop mythics can be equipped alongside one crafted — but drop rates are low and random
- This system may be revisited in a hotfix or future season — community pressure is building
