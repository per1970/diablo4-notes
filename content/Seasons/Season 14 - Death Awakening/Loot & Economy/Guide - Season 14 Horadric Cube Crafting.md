> Lord of Hatred expansion system · Mythic crafting NEW · Transfiguration endgame chase

## Unlocking

- Complete the **Lord of Hatred campaign** (Skovos Isles region)
- Permanently available in **Temis**, the expansion endgame hub
- Not seasonal — carries across all future seasons
- Short introduction quest unlocks the Cube station

## Core Materials

| Material | Source |
|---|---|
| **Raw Primordial Dust** (Tier 1) | Elites level 20+, War Plans, Tree of Whispers caches |
| **Coarse / Refined / Volatile / Attuned / Enhanced / Pure Dust** (Tiers 2–7) | War Plans, Whispers caches, elites at higher Torment |
| **Tuning Prisms** (8 types) | War Plans, Whispers caches, elites — see below |
| **Infused Horadric Resin** | Salvage Talisman Charms and Seals |
| **Pandemonium Fragments** | Season reputation, Resplendent Caches, Corrupted Reaper boss |

Primordial Dust is **account-bound**, cannot be traded. Six confirmed tiers, each rarer than the last. Higher tiers require Torment content.

## Tuning Prisms — Control Mechanic

Tuning Prisms bias Cube recipes toward chosen stat categories. **Not consumed on failure, consumed on success.**

| Prism | Affix Category |
|---|---|
| **Aggressive** | Mainstat, Weapon Dmg, Attack Speed, Crit Chance, Crit/Vuln/DoT/All/Elemental Damage [x], Thorns |
| **Pragmatic** | Barrier Gen, CDR, Fortify Gen, Healing, Impair Reduction, Life Regen, Lucky Hit, Movespeed, Potion, Evade |
| **Protector's** | Armor, DR, Dodge, Fortify Gen, Life on Hit/Kill, Max Life, All Res, Specific Res |
| **Resourceful** | Lucky Hit Restore Resource, Max Resource, Cost Reduction, Resource on Kill, Resource Regen |
| **Adept's** | Mainstat, Skill Ranks |
| **Chromatic** | Specific Resistances — **S14 change:** can now also target **All Resistance** (small chance) |
| **Entropic** | **Transfiguration** — removes worst AND best outcomes; 100% locks item |
| **Kullean** | **Transfiguration (Amulet only)** — adds bonus legendary aspect; does NOT lock item |

## All Recipes

### Gear Modification

| Recipe | Input | Materials | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Add Affix** | Common/Magic/Rare/Legendary | 1x Coarse + 5x Raw Dust + Prism (opt) | Adds random affix. Must be below affix limit. |
| **Chaotic Reroll** | Magic/Rare/Legendary | 1x Refined + 15x Raw Dust + Prism | Changes affix to random other category |
| **Focused Reroll** | Magic/Rare/Legendary | 1x Refined + 15x Raw Dust + Prism | Changes affix within same category (e.g. Crit Chance → Attack Speed) |
| **Remove Affix** | Magic/Rare only | 1x Refined + 15x Raw Dust + Prism | Removes random affix. Can strip to Normal/white. |
| **Transfigure Item** | Legendary/Unique/Mythic | 1x Volatile Dust + Prism (opt) | Major upgrade — see Transfiguration section |

### Item Transmutation

| Recipe | Input | Materials | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unique Power Reroll** | Any Unique | 1x Attuned + 100x Raw Dust | Rerolls unique power affix range. Works on Unique Charms now (S14). |
| **3-to-1 Transmutation** | 3x same item type | None | Random new item. Good for set charm hunting. |
| **Recycle Unique** | 3x same unique | None | Fresh roll of that unique. IP follows lowest used — match 900 IP items. |
| **Upgrade to Unique** | 1x Common item | 1x Enhanced + 10x Raw Dust | Normal gear → random unique of same slot. Strip blue/yellow to common first. |

## Transfiguration — The Final Step

**CRITICAL RULE:** Transfiguration is **always last.** Complete socket → temper → enchant → aspect → masterwork BEFORE transfiguring. Transfigured items cannot be modified further (except gem/rune swaps).

### Outcomes (no Prism, ~540 Barbarian samples)

| Outcome | ~Likelihood |
|---|---|
| **Indestructible** (no durability loss) | ~20% |
| **Upgrade to Greater Affix** | ~15% |
| **Bonus Transfiguration Affix** (extra affix) | ~35% |
| **Replace affix with Transfiguration Affix** | ~10% |
| **Bonus Item Quality** (1-15 Quality) | ~20% |

**~1/16 chance** item stays modifiable after transfiguration — enables a **second transfiguration**.

### Entropic Prism
- 100% locks item (unmodifiable)
- Removes both very bad AND very good outcomes
- Use only on irreplaceable one-off items

### Kullean Prism (Amulet only)
- Adds a **bonus legendary aspect** (second aspect slot)
- Does **NOT** lock the item
- Can keep re-rolling until desired aspect — extremely powerful endgame

### Transfiguration Affix Pool (highlights)

|---|---|

## Mythic Unique Crafting ⭐ NEW S14

### Recipe
- **Input:** 1x Unique item with **850+ Item Power**
- **Materials:** 5x **Pandemonium Fragments**
- **Output:** Random Mythic Unique for that slot
- **Requirement:** Level 70+, Torment difficulty

### Key Rules

1. **One crafted Mythic equipped at a time** — applies to Cube, Jeweler, AND Blacksmith crafting. Check tooltip for "Crafted" tag.
2. **Crafted Mythics always roll at 900 IP** (Ancestral).
3. **Unique Power gets +30% boost** on the resulting Mythic.
4. **All affixes roll at maximum value** on crafted Mythics.
5. **Slot is deterministic EXCEPT 1H/Offhand/Shield** — those three share a pool (RNG between them).
6. **Input unique is consumed** — output is random within slot. Do NOT feed a perfect 4GA unique expecting the same item.
7. Mythics can still be Enchanted, Tempered, and Masterworked.

### Pandemonium Fragment Sources
- **Season Reputation Board** — main reward track
- **Resplendent Caches**
- **Corrupted Reaper** — seasonal Lair Boss
- **Jeweler crafting** — 3 Resplendent Sparks + 18 specific runes (alternative)

## Material Farming

### Best Sources

| Source | What It Gives | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **NMD "Materials Reserve" affix** | Tuning Prisms, all dust tiers. Chests on minimap. | #1 |
| **Escalating NMDs** | Scaling rewards per floor. Floor 3 ~2x Floor 1. | #1 |
| **Undercity "Ingenuity" tribute** | Forces cube materials. Bargain options target specific Prisms. | #2 |
| **Tree of Whispers caches** | Passive dust + Prism income | #3 |
| **War Plans** | Reward nodes give cube materials | Passive |

### White/Blue Items Matter
- Common and Magic items are the **best crafting bases** in S14
- No existing affixes to conflict with — strip to Normal, build from scratch
- Do NOT auto-vendor grey/white items this season

### Progression Tips

1. **Early season:** "Upgrade to Legendary" on rares → fill aspect codex
2. **Mid season:** Strip yellow ancestrals → Common → "Upgrade to Unique" for slot-specific uniques
3. **Endgame:** Transfiguration LAST, after full masterwork/temper/enchant
4. **Mythic crafting:** Use spare uniques — don't gamble your main gear
5. **Charm optimization:** Reroll Set Charm until +3 desired skills + GAs
6. **Boss trophy amalgamation:** 5x trophies = target-farm uniques from specific bosses
