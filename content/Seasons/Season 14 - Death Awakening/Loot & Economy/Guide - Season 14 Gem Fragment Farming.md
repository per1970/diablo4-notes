## S14 Gem System Overhaul

Three major changes make gem farming dramatically faster than S13:

### 1. Lossless Conversion
- **Before S14:** Converting 3 gem types cost 300 shards → 200 output (40%+ loss)
- **S14:** Conversion costs exactly 200 → 200 output. **Zero loss.**
- **This means:** Farm ANY gem type and convert freely to what you need. No color chasing.

### 2. Proper Salvage Values
- Disenchanting high-level gems now returns **full value**
- Grand Gem salvages into **1,000,000 gem shards**
- No more risk of "ruining" progress by salvaging the wrong gem

### 3. Significantly Increased Fragment Drops
- Torment X+ sees massively increased shard drop rates
- Escalating NM Horadric chests: **250,000 fragments from a single chest**
- One boss kill can yield nearly an entire Grand Gem's worth

## THE META: Nightmare Dungeon "Gem Reserve"

### Method 1: Gem Reserve NMDs
- Look for NMD sigils with **"Gem Reserve"** affix (Ruby Reserve, Amethyst Reserve, etc.)
- Special chests spawn throughout the dungeon, shown on minimap
- wudijo's result: **~200,000 Ruby fragments** from one dungeon run
- Boss drops extra: ~500,000 total from a single run
- **Enough for 2 Grand Rubies** from one dungeon

### Method 2: Escalating NMD Horadric Chests (BEST)
- cliptis86's discovery: **250,000 fragments from a single Horadric chest**
- The deeper the escalation floor → larger the payout
- Escalating NM + Branching Pathways War Plan node = pick your reward type per floor
- One quarter of a Grand Gem (100k needed) from ONE chest

### Method 3: Combined
- Run Escalating NMD with Gem Reserve affix
- Open every Horadric chest + kill boss
- Result: **750,000+ fragments per run** (combined chests + boss)

## Cube Amalgamation for Top-Tier Gems

The **only way** to get Horadric and Flawless Horadric gems is Cube amalgamation:

| Step | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5x Grand (same type) | 1x Horadric |
| 2 | 5x Horadric (same type) | 1x Flawless Horadric |

Cannot be crafted at Jeweler — **Cube only.** With massive fragment drops from Escalating NM, getting Horadric+ gems is now reasonable.

## Practical Farming Route

1. **Roll NM sigils** until you hit a "Gem Reserve" affix (any color — lossless conversion means color doesn't matter)
2. **Prefer Escalating NM sigils** — deeper floors = bigger Horadric chest payouts
3. **Run on highest Torment** you can clear efficiently
4. **Open every Horadric chest** — shown on minimap
5. **Kill the boss** — extra fragment payout
6. **Salvage any Grand+ gems** if you need different types (full value return now)
7. **Convert all fragments** to Ruby (or your target type) — lossless
8. **Craft to Grand** at Jeweler, then **amalgamate to Horadric** in Cube
