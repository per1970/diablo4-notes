> [!d4-s14] Season 14 Gold Farming
> Overlapping Pandemonium Ruptures in Helltides is the new gold king — dethroning Infernal Hordes as the best all-around farm.

## TL;DR

**Overlapping Pandemonium Ruptures in Helltides is the best gold farm in S14.** It generates more vendorable gear per hour than any previous method, while simultaneously farming XP, materials, keys, Whisper reputation, and seasonal progress. Infernal Hordes remains the best *direct* gold conversion (Spoils of Gold), but as a total-value-per-hour activity it's been overtaken.

## The S14 Gold Meta Shift

Season 13's gold meta was straightforward: Infernal Hordes for raw gold, Helltides for accessibility. S14 flips this on its head with one mechanic:

> **Overlapping Pandemonium Ruptures in a Helltide create an infinite gear cascade.**

The chain reaction — ruptures spawn goblins, goblins spawn more ruptures, Butchers spawn from max Threat, Profaned Mindcages boost everything — produces 10-15 minutes of non-stop loot on a single spot. Every item is vendorable. The result is gold income that rivals or exceeds dedicated Hordes farming *while also* generating XP, keys, mats, cinders, and seasonal rep.

## Tier 1: Overlapping Pandemonium Ruptures (Helltide) — The New King

**Best overall gold farm in S14. Also the best XP farm, best mat farm, and best key farm.** If you're only doing one activity for wealth, this is it.

### How It Works

1. Enter a **Helltide** zone with high player density
2. Find **2-4 Pandemonium Ruptures** close together on the minimap
3. Activate them simultaneously → overlapping Rift zones stack monster density
4. Kill everything → goblins spawn more ruptures → chain reaction sustains **10-15 minutes**
5. **Meat Brothers** War Plan node spawns 2 Butchers at max Threat → drop **Profaned Mindcages**
6. Stack Mindcages → boosted monster power → more loot per kill
7. **Vendor everything.** With this gear density, vendoring alone out-earns Hordes Spoils of Gold

### War Plan Setup for Gold

| Tree | Points | Key Nodes |
|---|---|---|
| Helltide | 7/8 | **Meat Brothers** (mandatory), Bursting Brute, Night Terrors, Hellmouth, Writhe and Rot, Bursting Brood |
| Tree of Whispers | 5/8 min | **Corrupted Roots** (mandatory), Head Rotten Feast (mandatory), Roots of Power |
| Flexible | 1-2 | Tainted Shrines, Hated Foe — or fill Lair Boss for key bonuses |

**Corrupted Roots** is the gold multiplier: plant it inside your rupture overlap to attract even more enemies. **Head Rotten Feast** doubles the rewards from those attracted enemies in Helltides.

**Pro tip:** Swap the Whisperer point to XP bonus only when opening caches (store them up, open in bulk).

### The Material Cascade

Rob2628's chain-reaction loop produces all of this simultaneously:

- **Gold** — vendoring the endless gear stream
- **Forgotten Souls, Veiled Crystals, Obducite** — from salvaging what you don't sell
- **Profaned Mindcages** — from Butcher kills, stack for more rewards
- **Whisper reputation** — ~20 caches/hour, each giving gold lump sums + legendaries
- **Boss Lair Keys** — Greater Lair Keys, Living Steel
- **Gem Fragments** — plentiful in S14, Pandemonium Chests now drop them

Rob2628's haul on the way to Paragon 300: 300+ Greater Lair Keys, 2,000+ Living Steel, 500+ Profaned Mindcages, hundreds of Whisper Caches.

## Tier 2: Infernal Hordes — Best Direct Gold Conversion

**Still the best if you want to convert time into gold directly** — no vendoring RSI, no gear management. But as a total-value activity it loses to ruptures.

- Each run dumps a dense pile of gear for vendoring
- **Spoils of Gold** converts leftover Aether to gold directly
- High-Torment Hordes give scaled item drops that vendor for large sums
- Stacks with War Plan Activity Tree perks

> [!info] Build gating
> Requires a build that can clear T8+ Hordes efficiently. Below that, Helltide ruptures pull ahead even for direct gold.

**Veteran trick:** If you finish a Hordes run with sub-400 Aether (not enough for Greater Equipment), dump it all into Spoils of Gold. Aether evaporates between runs — gold doesn't.

## Tier 3: Tree of Whispers — Passive Gold Layer

Run on top of other content, not as a dedicated farm.

- Cache turn-ins give large gold lump sums
- Helltide ruptures naturally complete full 10-Grim-Favor cycles → free cache every Helltide
- With **Fortune or Famine**, caches are always Greater Quality or Junk → more gold per good cache
- Greater Collection of Chaos is the gold-densest cache choice

## The S14 Sell-vs-Salvage Rule

> [!warning] Stop reflexively salvaging — S14 makes it even worse
> With gem fragments now plentiful and rupture farming generating unprecedented gear volume, **the default should be selling**, not salvaging. Only salvage items with aspects you actively need for Codex upgrades.

| Need | Action |
|---|---|
| Gold | **Sell** |
| Specific aspect upgrade | Salvage that item |
| Materials | Salvage (but you get plenty from rupture chests + caches) |

Gem fragments are no longer a bottleneck in S14 — Blizzard boosted drop rates significantly. This removes the main reason people reflexively salvaged in S13. Set your loot filter to keep yellows visible and vendor them.

## The Optimal Gold Loop

1. **Build a War Plan** with Helltide (7 pts) + Whispers (5 pts) — Meat Brothers, Corrupted Roots, Head Rotten Feast are mandatory
2. **Enter a Helltide** at the highest Torment your build can clear deathlessly
   - Deaths cost cinders → efficiency loss
3. **Hunt overlapping ruptures** on the minimap → activate 2-4 simultaneously
4. **Plant Corrupted Roots** inside the overlap to pull more enemies
5. **Kill everything.** Let the chain reaction sustain itself. Stack Profaned Mindcages as they drop
6. **Grab Whisper caches** when you hit 10 Grim Favors (you will, constantly)
7. **Vendor everything yellow and orange** unless it has an aspect you need
8. **Open Whisper caches in bulk** — swap Whisperer point to XP bonus first
9. **When Helltide ends**, run any accumulated Lair Keys on bosses, then back to ruptures
