## Overview

Echoing Hatred is the wave-survival arena from Lord of Hatred. Fixed room, escalating waves, **no timer**. Run ends when the **Overwhelm bar** fills — it tracks live enemy count, not progress. Structurally similar to D3's Echoing Nightmare but with rarer access and a longer scaling curve.

## S14 Changes (Patch 3.1.0)

- **Maximum wave cap: 150** (was uncapped in S13)
- **Trace of Echoes drop rate from Elites/Champions increased** — also now drops as early as Torment I
- **Mythic Seal drop rate massively increased** (Wudijo had two in the first week; S13 players reported never seeing one)

## Access — Trace of Echoes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | Any loot source (barrels, chests, monsters, bosses, events) |
| **Best farms** | Endgame bosses, Helltide Mystery Chests, Pit completions, Infernal Hordes |
| **Drop rate** | <5% from endgame sources. Much better than S13 — players report drops from random Torment I dungeons |
| **Storage** | Inventory item. **No expiry, no cap.** Stockpile and run when your build is ready |
| **Entry point** | **Sightless Eye** — northeast area of Temis, Skovos. Interact to consume a Trace and teleport in |

## Overwhelm Bar Mechanics

The bar on the right side of your screen tracks **live enemy count**, not run progress. As enemies spawn faster than you clear them, the bar fills. When it caps, the run ends. Killing enemies shrinks it.

**This is the defining mechanic.** A build that one-shots bosses but takes 20 seconds to clear 30 trash mobs will fail deep runs. **AoE crowd-clear is the dominant build axis.**

## Tier Scaling

- Difficulty climbs **+1 tier per cleared wave**
- Ignores your selected World Tier — every run starts at base difficulty
- Tier 50 ≈ Torment V, Tier 100 ≈ Torment X, cap at 150
- Torment VII+ separates meta builds from off-meta

## Build Priority

In strict priority order:

1. **AoE damage** — the Overwhelm bar punishes anything else
2. **Survivability** — enemy density spikes hard at higher tiers
3. **Single-target** — boss waves arrive on top of trash
4. **Mobility** — useful but not load-bearing

**Avoid single-target specialists entirely** (Twisting Blades Rogue, Heart of the Hunter setups). Glass-cannon builds hit walls early.

## Treasure Goblins

Treasure Goblins spawn during runs. Each one you kill drops a **dedicated Mythic Unique loot bag** at run end. The brief Overwhelm risk from chasing one is always worth it — they're the primary Mythic source from Echoing Hatred.

## Rewards

Loot scales with depth. Deeper waves = better odds.

| Reward | Notes |
|---|---|
| Legendary items | Always drop. Quantity scales with depth |
| Unique items | Reliable beyond early tiers. Higher tiers shift pool toward expansion-exclusive uniques |
| Mythic Uniques | Decent chance if you push deep. Primary source: Treasure Goblin bags |
| Talismans (Seals + Charms) | One of the most efficient Talisman farms in the game. S14 buffed Mythic Seal drops |
| Crafting materials | Veiled Crystals, Forgotten Souls, Obducite, Horadric Cube components |
| XP + Paragon | Substantial — competitive with Helltide / Infernal Hordes per Trace spent |
| Gold | Scaling end-of-run lump sum |

## Strategy TL;DR

1. **Hoard Traces** — don't burn them on weak builds. No expiry, no cap
2. **Bring AoE + survivability** — this is the entire assignment
3. **Kill every Treasure Goblin** — Mythic bags are the prize
4. **Push as deep as you can** — rewards scale with depth, not speed
5. **Traces drop everywhere now** — you'll get them naturally while farming other content
