## Fastest XP: Overlapping Pandemonium Rifts (Helltide)

Rob2628 hit **Paragon 300 in 56h 50m** using Whirlwind Barb. This is the fastest Paragon 300 ever recorded.

### How It Works

1. Enter a **Helltide** zone with high player density
2. Look for **overlapping Pandemonium Ruptures** on the minimap (2-4 rifts in close proximity)
3. Activate all overlapping rifts simultaneously → massive monster density chain
4. Goblins from one rift open another rift → chains lasting **10-15 minutes** on a single spot
5. **Meat Brothers** War Plan node spawns 2 Butchers per Hellborn event → drop **Profane Mindcages**
6. Stack Mindcages (Rob2628 had 350+) → boosted monster power → more XP per kill
7. During optimal overlap session: **~50 million XP per pop**

### War Plan Setup for Overlapping Rifts

| Tree | Points | Key Nodes |
|---|---|---|
| Helltide | 7/8 | **Meat Brothers** (mandatory), Bursting Brute, Night Terrors |
| Tree of Whispers | 5/8 min | Head Rotten Feast, Roots of Power, Fortune & Fame |
| Flexible | 1 | Last point optional |

**Pro tip:** Switch Whisperer point to XP bonus only when opening caches (store them up).

**Key Pit insight:** Pit XP is level-based, not Torment-based. Pit 100 in < 2 min > Pit 145 in 3 min. Speed > level.

## Realmwalkers & Deathtoll Chambers

### Realmwalker Event
- Seasonal world event (spawns same time as Legion events, near Zarbinzet)
- Creates **3 Pandemonium Ruptures** beside each other
- Kill until they expand and merge → spawns **Realmwalker boss** (guaranteed)
- Most reliable way to target the Realmwalker → portal to **Deathtoll Chamber**

### Deathtoll Chamber
- Mini-dungeon containing a single large Rupture + special goblin mechanics
- **Primary source of Superior Lair Keys** (replaced old Belial key material)
- Superior Lair Keys used for: **Corrupted Reaper** (new seasonal boss, enhanced Mythic drop rates) and **Belial**
- Best in slot for farming Mythic Unique materials and Resplendent Sparks

### Surging Ruptures (Helltide)
- Moving Tears require repositioning
- Chance to end with Realmwalker → Deathtoll Chamber portal
- Pandemonium Chests during events now drop Gem Fragments (scales on Torment)

## "Infinite Mats" — How It Works

Rob2628's material cascade comes from overlapping rift chains:

1. Each Pandemonium Rupture spawns monsters + chance for **goblin**
2. Killing goblin spawns **another rupture**
3. Multiple overlapping rifts → multiple simultaneous goblin chance rolls → chain reaction
4. Materials gained: Forgotten Souls, Veiled Crystals, Obducite, Profane Mindcages, Whisper rep, Gem Fragments, Boss Lair Keys
5. Whirlwind Barb's vacuum pull + screen-wide AoE sustains chains 10-15 min non-stop
6. **~20 Whisper Caches/hour** → each gives multiple legendaries + materials

**Rob2628's haul in 57h to P300:** 300+ Greater Lair Keys, 2,000+ Living Steel, 500+ Profane Mindcages, hundreds of Whisper Caches, multiple mythics.

## Endgame Progression Path (Icy Veins)

1. **Hit 70** → World Boss cache, Obol gambling for quick 850+ gear
2. **Horadric Cube** → upgrade normal items to uniques
3. **Set Charms** → drop more at T3, reroll in Cube to complete sets
4. **Paragon Legendary Nodes** → rush these first (60-100% damage each) — bigger boost than glyphs
5. **Season Journey** → skill/paragon points, gear caches, key caches, boss keys
6. **Deathtoll Chamber** → Superior Lair Keys → Belial + Corrupted Reaper → Mythic Uniques
