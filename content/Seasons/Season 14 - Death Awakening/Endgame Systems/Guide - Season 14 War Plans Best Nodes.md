> [!d4-s14] Season 14 War Plans
> Season of Death Awakening · Party Sync added · Maggot XP farming is the leveling meta

## What Changed in Season 14

- **Party Sync**: War Plans can now be synchronized across your party. Costs 2 Marks of El'Druin to create a synced plan. Opening War Plans grants 3 Marks by default — you can sync immediately.
- **Maggot farming**: Hellwyrm → Maggot XP engine is the dominant leveling strategy (see Helltide section below).
- **No major tree restructure** from S13 — same activity trees, but the meta around which nodes to prioritize has shifted due to new seasonal mechanics and Mythic Unique 3.0 economy.

Respec any tree anytime with gold. No locking.

## Party Sync Mechanics (New)

1. All party members must be in Temis
2. One player creates a synced War Plan (costs 2 Marks of El'Druin)
3. The party gets a shared board — everyone progresses the same objectives
4. When the party disbands, each player keeps their individual progress plus synced rewards

**Priority:** Sync before any serious group farming session. The 10% party XP bonus + shared cinders + synced War Plans multiply each other.

## Activity-by-Activity Node Rankings

### 1. Helltide (Highest Priority — Leveling + Economy)

The most important activity in S14. You'll spend most of 1-70 here, and it stays relevant in endgame for boss mats and Mythic Unique farming.

**Best Path: Middle column**

| Node | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Hellmouth** | Mid | Hellwyrm unleashes one of: Chaos Rift (rewards), Maggots (XP orbs), or Bloodseekers (Ancestral drops) |
| **Writhe and Rote** | Top | Hellwyrms **always** spew Maggots (+1 Monster Power). Chance to spawn Pang of Duriel (drops Lair Keys) |
| **Bursting Blood** | Bottom | Maggots and Pangs **explode** on death, frequently dropping Boss Trophies |

**Why this order:** Hellmouth enables the mechanic. Writhe and Rote makes it reliable — guaranteed Maggots every Hellwyrm. Bursting Blood adds boss mats on top of XP. This is the S14 leveling engine.

**Priority:** Rush these three nodes before anything else if you're leveling.

### 2. Kurast Undercity (Leveling Engine Post-25)

Unlocks at level 25. Becomes the primary XP source after you unlock Jade Epiphany.

**Best Path: Left column**

| Node | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Endless Swarm** | Mid | Invasion Portals appear, enemies grant Attunement |
| **Unfortunate Souls** | Mid | Wisps attach to you — each saved Wisp rewards Forgotten Souls + crafting mats at end |
| **Gutter Filth** | Mid | Portal Pranksters + Rats from beacons/portals |
| **Trials and Tributes** | Bottom | Each Portal Prankster killed = Tribute Chest after District Boss |
| **Jade Epiphany** | Key | XP upgrade — scales with difficulty. Higher difficulty = more XP |

**Strategy:** At 25, gamble 2/3 War Plans toward Undercity. Rush to the boss in your first few runs to unlock Jade Epiphany fast. Once you have it, Undercity becomes your #2 XP engine (behind Helltide Maggots).

Forgotten Souls from Unfortunate Souls are critical for crafting — this tree provides both XP and crafting economy.

### 3. Nightmare Dungeons (Goblin Economy)

**Best Path: Right column**

| Node | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Goblinfall** | Mid | Treasure Goblins can drop Nightmare Sigils with Treasure Breach affix |
| **Horadric Hunter** | Mid | Kill 25 Elites → Horadric Altar spawns. Random outcome: Elites, Goblins, The Butcher, Riches, or Terrible Misfortune |
| **Altar of Avarice** | Top | Horadric Altars → Goblin Altars (only summon Treasure Goblins) |
| **Greed is Good** | Bottom | Killing a Goblin in NMD has small chance to spawn portal to Goblin's Retreat |

**Priority:** Medium. Great for gold and loot, but Helltide + Undercity are better for XP. Farm NMDs when you need glyph XP or want a break from Helltides.

Goblin's Retreat is rare — don't build your strategy around it, treat it as a bonus.

### 4. Tree of Whispers (Economy)

**Best Path: Middle column**

| Node | Tier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Tree of Plenty** | Mid | Tree offers a 4th Whisper Cache choice |
| **Fortune or Famine** | Mid | Whisper Caches are always either Greater Quality... or Junk (binary) |
| **Grim Mysteries** | Mid | Collections of Chaos → Collections of Mysteries (can drop anything) |
| **Resplendent Favor** | Bottom | Each Whisper Cache has a chance to drop a **Resplendent Spark** |

**Priority:** Resplendent Favor is the payoff — free Resplendent Sparks from cache openings. Sparks are used for Mythic Unique crafting. Get this before you start serious cache farming.

The 4th cache choice (Tree of Plenty) is quality-of-life — more options = better odds of getting what you need.

### 5. Lair Bosses (Endgame)

**Best Path: Middle column**

Focus on nodes that add extra boss portals and loot multipliers. S14 adds the Corrupted Reaper (seasonal boss in Zarbinzet, Torment I+), which has the best Mythic Unique drop rates of any activity.

**Priority:** Low while leveling. Become high priority post-70 when you start Mythic Unique farming. Boss mats come naturally from Helltide and Undercity farming — you'll have a stockpile by the time you hit 70.

## Leveling Priority Order (1-70)

```
1. Helltide — Hellmouth → Writhe and Rote → Bursting Blood
2. Undercity — Endless Swarm → Jade Epiphany (@25)
3. Nightmare Dungeons — Goblinfall → Altar of Avarice (when you want variety)
4. Tree of Whispers — Resplendent Favor (when you have spare points)
```

## Endgame Priority Order (70+, Torment+)

```
1. Helltide — Bursting Blood (boss mats for Mythic farming)
2. Lair Bosses — middle path (extra portals, more boss kills)
3. Undercity — Trials and Tributes + Jade Epiphany (XP + crafting mats)
4. Tree of Whispers — Resplendent Favor (Resplendent Sparks)
5. Nightmare Dungeons — Greed is Good (glyph XP + goblin bonuses)
```

## Key Synergies

- **Helltide Maggots → Boss Trophies (Bursting Blood)** → Lair Boss farming → Mythic Uniques
- **Undercity Jade Epiphany + Helltide farming** → dual XP engines stack
- **Party Sync + Group XP bonus + Shared cinders** → multiply each other when coordinated
