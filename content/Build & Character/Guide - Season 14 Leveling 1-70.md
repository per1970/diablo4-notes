> [!d4-s14] Season 14 Leveling
> Season of Death Awakening · Launched June 30, 2026 · Level cap 70

## Quick Route Summary

```
Normal/Hard → Seasonal Setup → War Plans → Helltide (Maggot farming) → 
Undercity @25 (Jade Epiphany) → 70 → Pit & Torment+
```

The single biggest S14 change for leveling: **Hellwyrms spew Maggots that drop XP orbs** via the Writhe and Rote War Plan upgrade. This is the fastest XP engine pre-25. Post-25, Undercity's Jade Epiphany takes over.

## Early Setup Checklist (First 5 Minutes)

| Order | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enter Seasonal Mode | Required for S14 progression |
| 2 | Claim early Seasonal rewards | Fast early level boost (~level 2) |
| 3 | Unlock War Plans at Command Table | Starts seasonal progression |
| 4 | Pick up Mercenaries | Damage, defense, utility |
| 5 | Select a pet (Wardrobe) | Auto-collects gold and materials |
| 6 | Join Party Finder | 10% XP bonus, faster clears |

Do this BEFORE running into any content. You'll be level 2 before your first kill.

## Difficulty: Speed Over Multiplier

| Kill Speed | Recommended Difficulty |
|---|---|
| Packs die instantly | Bump up one tier |
| 2-3 seconds per pack | Stay here |
| Bosses feel slow | Drop one tier |
| Strong group, fast clears | Penitent worth it |

**Rule:** Start on Hard. Drop to Normal if you notice any slowdown. Penitent only if you're in a group melting everything. XP multipliers mean nothing if your kills-per-hour drops.

## War Plan Priorities for Leveling

### Pre-25: Rush Helltide War Plans

Fish for two Helltide War Plans at level 1. The goal is **Writhe and Rote** — this makes Hellwyrms always spew Maggots that burst into XP orbs. Hellwyrms also have a chance to spawn a **Pang of Duriel**, which drops Lair Keys when killed.

Path: Hellmouth → Writhe and Rote → Bursting Blood

Bursting Blood makes Maggots and Pangs explode on death, often dropping Boss Trophies. Free boss mats while leveling.

### Post-25: Gamble Undercity War Plans

At level 25, Kurast Undercity unlocks. Gamble 2/3 of your War Plans to be Undercity-related. Rush to the boss in your first few runs to unlock **Jade Epiphany** — this upgrade scales XP with difficulty. Higher difficulty = more XP per Jade Epiphany proc.

Once you have both Writhe and Rote (Helltide) + Jade Epiphany (Undercity), you have the two strongest XP engines active simultaneously.

## Level 1-25: Helltide Hellwyrm Farming

Primary activity: **Helltides**. Secondary: open-world events, easy dungeons.

- Kill everything inside Helltides — density is highest here
- Chase Hellwyrm spawns — they trigger Maggot waves with Writhe and Rote
- Stay within 2-3 screens of your party in Helltide (cinder sharing, XP sharing)
- **Cinder turn-in trick**: farm cinders on your comfortable difficulty, then swap to Penitent before opening chests for a big XP spike. Only worth it if you won't die and lose cinders.

Gear priorities at this stage: highest item power weapon > armor with life/resistance > any legendary aspect that buffs your main skill. Don't min-max — replace and move.

## Level 25-70: Undercity + Helltides

Once Undercity opens:

1. Run Undercity with Jade Epiphany active — rush boss, repeat
2. Do Helltides whenever they're active (Maggot XP + Undercity XP stack)
3. Nightmare Dungeons with a group for variety and glyph XP
4. Infernal Hordes if your wave clear is smooth (medium priority)

**Activity priority order:**
1. Helltide (always when active — Maggots + cinders)
2. Kurast Undercity (Jade Epiphany runs)
3. Nightmare Dungeons (group, glyph XP)
4. Infernal Hordes (if clear speed is good)

Do **not** farm Lair Bosses or The Pit before 70 — they're endgame activities.

## Group Play Benefits (Season 14)

- **10% total XP bonus** (5% nearby + 5% partied)
- **Shared cinders** in Helltide — stay close
- **Shared NMD XP** — entire dungeon, even if split up for objectives
- **War Plans party sync** — new in S14. Costs 2 Marks of El'Druin to create a synced party plan. Opening War Plans gives 3 Marks by default, so you can sync immediately.

**Helltide group rule:** stay within 2-3 screens. Splitting across the zone kills efficiency for everyone.

## Post-70 Transition

At 70:
- Enter **Torment I+** difficulty
- Start **The Pit** for glyph leveling
- Farm **Lair Bosses** (Torment+ required)
- Seasonal Lair Boss: **Corrupted Reaper** in Zarbinzet (requires Betrayer's Husks from Deathtoll Chamber)
- Begin Mythic Unique farming (any Unique can drop as Mythic quality in S14)

## Alt Boosting

Fastest way to boost an alt: get invited by a geared player, they open a high Pit level, you wait at entrance and collect XP while they clear. Group bonus stacks.
